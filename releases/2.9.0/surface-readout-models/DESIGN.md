# Design: the surface read-out in the Analytic, the 3D solid and the Lumped (items e1, e22; the transient e23 last)

Branch `feature/surface-readout-models` from `development` (db6244b1). Source of the rules: `research/surface-readout-models` (README 4 and 7, INTEGRATION.md 1 to 7, `lib/rules.py`) and the reviewer's decisions quoted below. The surface is a VIEW (CLAUDE.md 11): it changes what is read from a solved field, never the solve; no number of any column, peak or mean read-out moves.

Wording rule (the audit's error pattern is an 'upper bound' that is not one): the Analytic and the Lumped carry their own column error into the number, so a surface there is `column + term`, never better than the column; the parabola is an upper estimate for t/a > 0.5; the Lumped value is a bound on the TERM, measured, never on a temperature.

## 2. The part over a copper window in the Analytic (the check the research asked for)

The Analytic does **not** read `laminate.face_terms` (no `face_terms` in `analytic.py`): its column spreads the copper of each layer into one uniform heater-weighted sheet. Measured on the feature branch (60 x 40 mm, 2 layers, 35 um, 6 x 6 mm 2 W part over a 10 x 10 mm window of the top copper, cell 1 mm): FVM column and surface peak 287.3 degC (the face term is in both), Analytic column 109.6 degC and surface 109.6 degC (1.8 mK above its column, the copper term of the full-copper sheet). So the Analytic surface under a part over a window is the sheet's, with the limits of the column; it is flagged by `surface_part_over_bare_laminate` (part footprint with copper fill < 1) and the model's own warning about copper coverage already says to use the FVM. **Review decision**: a surface 1.8 mK above a column 178 K below the FVM is not shown beside the FVM's 287.3 degC: that face of the Analytic is n/a (arrays NaN, no `t_max_surface_*` key; the reason in the report line and the part-table tooltip, `thickness.surface_na_reason`); the other face keeps its value.

## 3. Frozen numbers

The frozen records of 2.8.0 store the surface statistics of the FVM, FDM and FEM. The Analytic and 3D solid records have none, so their surface values are **new metrics of existing records**: the mechanism exists (`tests/regression/make_pending.py --new-readouts --model analytic ...`: a pending file with `"new_readouts"`, the metrics a frozen record does not hold are ADDED, no frozen number moves). Nothing is invented. The 3D solid records are also in `solid3d-pad-inplane.json` (another pending file): the run for the solid is a heavy script (listed with `--dry-run`, not run while the maintainer's battery runs).
