# Reproduction pass of 2.9.0: the reviewer's decision on scope, parallelism, stale tests and shifts

Asked by the main session at the 'reproduction pass' step of the release gate (CLAUDE.md section 12: open decisions go to the reviewer). Tree under test: `development` b241b15e; reference: the same scripts on the `pcbthermal/` of the tag `v2.8.0`. Measured by the reviewer before deciding: the changed modules (`git diff --stat v2.8.0..b241b15e -- pcbthermal`, 46 files), the CHANGELOG items that move numbers on purpose, the four pending files of `tests/regression/data/pending/`, the 40 scripts already compared, the load of the machine.

## 0. Defects of the pass itself (corrected by the reviewer)

1. **`scripts/compare_logs.py` masked the numbers that 2.9.0 moves on purpose.** Its normaliser replaced every line holding the word "time" (any case) and every number followed by `s`, `sec`, `min`: the simulated times, the "time to 63 %" lines and every transient line naming a time were removed before the comparison, so a transient study would have read "identical" whatever it printed. Rewritten: only paths, memory addresses, traceback line numbers and lines of wall-clock timing (`elapsed`, `took`, `run time`, `runtime`, `cpu time`, `wall clock`, `solved in`, `ran in`) are masked. A wall-clock column the mask misses shows as a `numeric` difference that the tester recognises by hand (one so far: `analytic-edge-spatial/study_strip_sensitivity`, the last column `0.05 s` against `0.04 s`), never the other way round. The 40 scripts compared so far are re-classified with the new version (result now: 28 identical, 6 of them the same error on both trees; 11 timeouts on the tree; 1 wall-clock column).
2. **`compare_logs.py`, `repro_scripts.sh` and `repro_tests.sh` had no licence header** (and the Python no Doxygen docstrings): `tests/test_license_header.py` and `tests/test_docs.py` failed on the branch (3 failed), so the whole battery on the merged tree would have been red. Headers and docstrings added; both tests pass (925 passed).
3. New helpers: `scripts/select_scripts.py` (the scope of point 1, derived mechanically) and `scripts/old_stepping/sitecustomize.py` (the explanation check of point 4). Checked: on `examples/example_board.pcbt.json` (t_end 200 s, 10 frames, cell 2 mm) the FVM and FDM transients of the tree with the shim are bit-identical (SHA-256 of the frames) to v2.8.0, peak 79.693 / 79.027 °C, while the tree alone gives 81.925 / 81.255 °C (the new stepping: hotter early frames, toward the fine-step reference).

The other modules that changed (`api`, `analysis`, `analytic`, `lumped`, `checks`, `thickness`, `plots`, `profiles`, `results`, `memory`, `compare`, `defaults`) are imported by nearly every study, so an import filter selects almost everything; every CHANGELOG item on them is "no number moves" (texts, warnings, view, exports), which the selected scripts check on the way (they print the steady FVM, FDM, Analytic and Lumped numbers too) and the sample checks where nothing else does. The scripts already compared stay compared.

**Timeouts**: 11 of the first 40 scripts timed out at 75 s and are therefore NOT reproduced. Use 600 s for T and F, 300 s for SB, S and sample. A T or F script that still times out is run once alone without a cap; any other timeout is listed as "not reproduced (time)" with the README number it would have checked. A script that fails with the same error on both trees ("identical error", 6 so far) is a stale study, not a reproduction: list it under its own heading with the error line.

## 2. Parallelism: one job now, a second and a third only while the rule holds

At the decision the swap was **2.50 GB used of 4 GB** (it had grown about 1.95 GB since the tester's measure of 0.55 GB), load 2.9, free + inactive about 9.9 GB. The swap rule of CLAUDE.md A10 (used < 2.5 GB and grown < 1.5 GB) does not hold, so the pass continues with **one job**. Before each folder the tester measures `uptime`, `sysctl vm.swapusage`, `vm_stat`: a second job starts when the swap is below 2.5 GB and load < 5 and free + inactive > 6 GB plus the job's peak; a third under the same rule. At most one F or SB-heavy folder (FEM with pyamg, 3D solid) at a time; the others take T, S and sample folders. Every job sets `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2 VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=2` (three jobs of all-core BLAS would push the load past 5 on their own) and keeps whole folders (scripts of one folder write into the same `out/`). **Pause rule**: load > 8 or swap > 2.5 GB, stop the youngest job after its current script and resume it when the rule holds again. Each job writes its own TSV (`repro_scripts_<class>_<n>.tsv`) so the jobs never append to one file.

**Estimated time**: the first 39 scripts measured took about 42 s per script (tree plus base) with the 75 s cap; with the longer caps about 70 s on average, so the scripts of the subset still to run (about 220) need about 4 to 5 hours at one job, about 2.5 hours at two and 2 hours at three.

## 3. The five stale research tests: stale by design, reproduced on their own tree, refreshed after the release on their own branches

Collection: the whole battery runs `pytest tests` and the repository has no `pytest.ini`, `pyproject.toml`, `setup.cfg` or `conftest.py` at its root, so no `research/*/tests` is collected by the battery (`research/theory/tests` and `research/transient-published-case/tests` neither; the battery reaches research only through `tests/test_docs.py` and tests that import a study script). The five failures therefore do not touch the battery and do not block the release.

Rule: a research folder is the record of what was measured on its tree. Its tests are NOT edited on the release branch and NOT skipped with a marker (a skip would hide the day they break for a real reason). In the pass each one is **reproduced on its tree of record** and recorded as "stale by design against 2.9.0, item X"; the refresh is done after the release on the folder's own `research/<topic>` branch (reopened, `development` merged first), by an executor, with a reviewer, as CLAUDE.md section 6 'No branch for its own sake' asks.

## 4. What is an unexplained shift, and what the tester does with each

The classes of `compare_logs.py`: identical, numeric (same words, numbers differ, largest relative difference), text (words differ). Rules:

1. **Bit-identical is required** for every number of the steady FVM, FDM, Analytic and Lumped, of the step responses and times to 63 % (`fvm.step_responses` is unchanged), of the 3D solid on boards with 35 um copper and no block, and for every script of the classes S and sample. Any numeric difference there is **unexplained**: the tester runs the pair again once (to rule out a nondeterminism), and if it stays, it is a defect candidate reported with the script, the line, both values and the commit range that touched the module (`git log v2.8.0..b241b15e -- <module>`).
2. **FVM and FDM transient frames** (class T, animation, `transient`, `solve_transient`): a shift is EXPLAINED only when the script, run on the tree with `PYTHONPATH=<worktree>/releases/2.9.0/reproduction/scripts/old_stepping:<tree>`, prints the shifted lines **bit-identical** to v2.8.0. Expected direction and size (CHANGELOG, example board): early frames hotter (the 2.8.0 first frame was 12 to 17 % low), the shift falling below about 1 % of the rise after the first seconds and to the last digits at the steady end. A shift that the shim does not remove is unexplained.
3. **FEM** (class F): FEM steady numbers may move only on a board with a part on an outer copper layer: the column by at most about 1 mK on the harness boards, the surface by up to the term q t / 2k, up to 1.5 K on a part straddling a corner without top copper (`drawn_ell`), as in the pending file `fem-thin-copper-source`. FEM transient output is new (no v2.8.0 counterpart): text class, explained as "added". A larger FEM shift, or one on a board without a part on outer copper, is unexplained.
4. **3D solid** (class SB): may move only on a board with a metal block (part under a top block up to about 0.9 % hotter at 5e-3 m2K/W; a bottom block a few mK, pending `solid3d-pad-inplane`) or with copper above 105 um (copper slabs: the column and the part numbers of that board). Anything else is unexplained.
5. **Text differences**: explained only when the new or changed lines are a warning, a label or a read-out listed in the CHANGELOG of 2.9.0 (the LPTN copper-coverage warning, the campaign warnings, the surface read-out added to the Analytic and the 3D solid, the read-out names on tables, the reference model named) AND every number common to both outputs is identical or explained by rules 2 to 4. A new error or traceback on the tree that v2.8.0 does not have is a defect candidate.

For every shift the tester writes one row: script, class, rule applied, explained (item) or unexplained. It does not change code; an unexplained shift is reported to the main session, which sends it to the reviewer before the pass is closed. The pass closes with 0 unexplained shifts, or with each one turned into a fix or an accepted limit by the maintainer.
