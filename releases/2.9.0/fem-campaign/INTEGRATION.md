# Integration: what the campaign found and proposes

Nothing here changes the program; the maintainer decides, a `fix/` or `feature/` branch implements (2.10 or a patch). Tree of every number: the program of `development` 7886ac42; tables in `tables/`, sections 56 and 57 of `docs/validation.md`.

1. **The Lumped is silent where it is 11 to 16 % cool** (`plate_pad`, `one_layer_pad`: -11.4 and -6.7 % of the FEM peak at 1 mm, -15.5 and -10.7 % of the independent layered solver, `tables/c1_steady_all_models.md`; the run printed no warning). The Analytic warns on the same boards ('copper coverage varies around the heaters'). Proposal: the same check (a part over a copper window with bare laminate round it) for the Lumped, with the measured sizes. Not measured: other window sizes.
2. **The default FEM mesh under-resolves a 20 Hz wave**: swing 4.90 K (the converged-in-time FEM 5.01 K) against 6.3 K of the independent axisymmetric solver (quoted from `research/fast-wave-oscillation`) and 6.07 K with 12 elements across a gap (16.3 s against 6.5 s). The layered models read 0.75 K (a documented limit with its own closed form for the decision). Proposal: a warning in a FEM profile run when the shortest stretch is below `2 (first gap element)^2 / alpha` of `docs/theory.md` 4b (the rule that already exists for step responses), or a finer gap mesh chosen by it. One board measured.
3. **The FEM step response of the example board took 175 s** here against 82 s predicted by the law of `fem-transient/MEASURED.md`; other jobs of the campaign were running; not separated. If it repeats on a quiet machine the law's constant is optimistic (e32 of the plan asks the opposite for the steady direct limit).
4. **`drawn_ell`**: the FEM does not converge in the cell either (64.4 / 84.1 / 95.3 K at 2 / 1 / 0.5 mm), as the FVM, FDM and 3D solid; the warning proposed by `research/drawn-ell-fvm-fdm/INTEGRATION.md` should name the FEM too.
5. **The FEM surface minus column on `hatched_copper` is 0.00 K, the FVM, FDM and 3D solid +1.22 K** (1 mm); unexplained, small; a question for the surface read-out of hatched layers.
6. **A bare-laminate board in the first 3 s**: the FEM stays 4.8 % below the semi-infinite closed form at 3 s even with 12 elements across a gap; the cause was not separated (57.3).
7. **The 3D solid is 4.8 and 4.5 % hotter than the FEM on the two pad boards at 1 mm** and equals the independent layered solver there: its own cell error (2.2 % at 0.5 mm), silent. Text for the trust hints, not a defect.
8. **The frozen harness caps the FEM at 130 000 unknowns**; at 300 000 (this campaign) the 1 mm column is complete for every board and the FEM's records at 1 mm exist for the example, profile, reference and cut boards. Raising the cap in `regress_harness.py` would add FEM records to the frozen set (a pending file); not proposed for 2.9.0, noted.
9. **`research/transient-published-case/scripts/study_t3_and_plate.py` part B** labels two lines 'implicit Euler' and 'Crank-Nicolson' that print the same digits since 2.9.0 (the default is Crank-Nicolson with the start). A note for that folder (not edited from here).
10. **Tooltip and trust-hint numbers** (`docs/fem-campaign-validation`, the second part of item c): the sizes the FEM hints may quote are in 56.1 (0.60 to 0.93 % mean, 1.4 to 2.4 % largest of the FVM, FDM and 3D solid on 14 boards at 1 mm), 56.1.2 and 56.7.
