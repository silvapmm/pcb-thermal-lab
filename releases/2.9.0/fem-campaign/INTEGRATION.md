# Integration: what the campaign found and proposes

Nothing here changes the program; the maintainer decides, a `fix/` or `feature/` branch implements (2.10 or a patch). Tree of every number: the program of `development` 7886ac42; tables in `tables/`, sections 56 and 57 of `docs/validation.md`. Items 3, 5 and 6 were corrected by the closing review (an earlier 'not explained' was wrong for 5 and 6; 3 compared two different runs); the ranking for 2.9.0 is at the end.

1. **The Lumped is silent where it is 11 to 16 % cool** (`plate_pad`, `one_layer_pad`: -11.4 and -6.7 % of the FEM peak at 1 mm, -15.5 and -10.7 % of the independent layered solver, `tables/c1_steady_all_models.md`; the run printed no warning). The Analytic warns on the same boards ('copper coverage varies around the heaters'). Proposal: the same check (a part over a copper window with bare laminate round it) for the Lumped, with the measured sizes. Not measured: other window sizes.
2. **The default FEM mesh under-resolves a 20 Hz wave**: swing 4.90 K (the converged-in-time FEM 5.01 K) against 6.3 K of the independent axisymmetric solver (quoted from `research/fast-wave-oscillation`) and 6.07 K with 12 elements across a gap (16.3 s against 6.5 s). The layered models read 0.75 K (a documented limit with its own closed form for the decision). Proposal: a warning in a FEM profile run when the shortest stretch is below `2 (first gap element)^2 / alpha` of `docs/theory.md` 4b (the rule that already exists for step responses), or a finer gap mesh chosen by it. One board measured.
3. **The FEM step response of the example board took 175 s** (review: 174.7 s again with the machine otherwise quiet). **Corrected by the review: the 82 s it was compared with is another run** (a 40-frame `fem.solve_transient`, 10 stages, 52 steps), while `api.step_response` runs `fem.step_responses` with 18 stages (72 frames, 84 steps, 19 factorisations); for that run the law gives 142 s of stepping without the build, so the law is about 20 % short of a whole step response. Impact: none on a result (the law sets notes and the 600 s refusal of a profile run, never a step response). Proposal, small and optional (2.10 or with e32): count the stages of `step_responses` and the build when a FEM step response is announced or timed.
4. **`drawn_ell`**: the FEM is low at every cell (64.4 / 84.1 / 95.3 K at 2 / 1 / 0.5 mm, -38.8 / -20.1 / -9.4 % of the converged 105.2 K of the independent tool); unlike the FVM, FDM and 3D solid it rises monotonically, but slowly (review). The warning proposed by `research/drawn-ell-fvm-fdm/INTEGRATION.md` (an extra of 2.9.0, not yet implemented: no model warns on this board) should name the FEM too, with these numbers.
5. **The FEM surface minus column on `hatched_copper` is 0.00 K, the FVM, FDM and 3D solid +1.22 K** (1 mm). **Explained by the review, not a defect**: 1.22 K is the laminate face term `q t_cu/(2 k_z)` (3 W on 12 x 12 mm, 35 µm, 0.3 W/mK: 1.215 K) that the FVM, FDM and 3D solid add to the board-level surface over the openings of a partly filled layer (`docs/theory.md` 1.2); the FEM meshes a smeared fill as a mixed element with no opening, so its face term is that of copper. The part read-outs agree. A question of definition for the maintainer (should the FEM read the openings of a smeared hatch as the others do?), no number of a part changes; low priority.
6. **A bare-laminate board in the first 3 s**: the FEM was 4.8 % below the semi-infinite closed form at 3 s even with 12 elements across a gap. **Explained by the review, a defect of the study's reference, not of the FEM**: the plain form is the face of a half-space without a film, while the models read the column of the top 35 µm layer (a constant `q t_cu/(2k)` below the face), lose heat through `h_top` and spread sideways; with the three closed-form factors (`tables/t3_first_seconds.md` T3c) the FEM with 12 gap elements is -0.16 to -0.21 % of the reference from 0.1 to 3 s and the default mesh -3.4 / -1.5 / -0.7 / -0.4 % at 0.1 / 0.3 / 1 / 3 s (the gap-mesh limit of `docs/theory.md` 4b). Nothing to change in the program.
7. **The 3D solid is 4.8 and 4.5 % hotter than the FEM on the two pad boards at 1 mm** and equals the independent layered solver there: its own cell error (2.2 % at 0.5 mm), silent. Text for the trust hints, not a defect. (Review: the 'pad' of these boards is a copper window, not the interface pad of a metal block, so `fix/solid3d-pad-inplane` does not touch them: its pending file moves only `finned_block`; the 3D solid converges from above and the FEM from below to limits within 0.15 %, 56.1.1.)
8. **The frozen harness caps the FEM at 130 000 unknowns**; at 300 000 (this campaign) the 1 mm column is complete for every board and the FEM's records at 1 mm exist for the example, profile, reference and cut boards. Raising the cap in `regress_harness.py` would add FEM records to the frozen set (a pending file); not proposed for 2.9.0, noted.
9. **`research/transient-published-case/scripts/study_t3_and_plate.py` part B** labels two lines 'implicit Euler' and 'Crank-Nicolson' that print the same digits since 2.9.0 (the default is Crank-Nicolson with the start; its 'Euler' runs pass no `theta_scheme`). A note for that folder (not edited from here). Its conclusion still holds at the new default: the 0.3 to 0.5 % of the held-edge plate is the logarithmic grid (review, validation 57.0: 40 linear frames of 16 steps read 0.01 to 0.08 %).
10. **Tooltip and trust-hint numbers** (`docs/fem-campaign-validation`, the second part of item c): the sizes the FEM hints may quote are in 56.1 (0.60 to 0.93 % mean, 1.4 to 2.4 % largest of the FVM, FDM and 3D solid on 14 boards at 1 mm; 0.15 to 0.30 % mean, 0.46 to 0.77 % largest at 0.5 mm), 56.1.2 and 56.7. Review: the hints (`checks.trust_hint`) do not exist yet; they come with `feature/compare-differences` (item b), so this text branch follows b. Files it will touch: `pcbthermal/checks.py` (hint texts), `pcbthermal/gui/tips.py` (the FEM tooltips), `pcbthermal/help/index.html` and `docs/USER_MANUAL.md` ('Which model to use'), with `tests/test_tooltips.py` and `tests/test_help.py`.
