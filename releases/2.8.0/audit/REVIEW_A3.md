## 2. Findings, ranked

1. **Unexplained discrepancy between two independent references of the contact cases (unexplained result).** The review of 2.6.0 quoted an independent 3D reference in `validation.md` 34.2 (quarter 5.70 K, track 10.34 / 10.89 K) from a scratch script that is not in the repository. The committed reference of this review (`study_via_contact_resolved.py`, converged to about 1 %) reads 7.91 K and 13.60 K: 39 % and 25 % higher; the via outside the edge agrees within 10 % and the full plane is not in the first. The likely cause (a staircase of whole cells scaled to the annulus area joins cells by their corners and cuts the path round the ring, which matters only where the heat enters part of the ring) was not tested. **Consequence**: the FVM reads low in both (safe side), but the **FDM of 2.7.1 reads the quarter at 0.5 mm 6.14 K, +7.7 % against the first reference (the unsafe side) and -22 % against the committed one**. Settling it needs the finite-element tool (item P2).
2. **Stale FDM numbers in the contact table (stale document).** The FDM column of `validation.md` 34.2 (quarter 2.39 / 3.89 / 4.75 K, track 2.26 / 3.74 / 5.76 K) is the FDM before the faces of 2.7.0; the tree prints 5.13 / 6.14 / 5.74 and 3.68 / 6.32 / 8.13. The warning text derived from it ('0 to 76 % in the FDM') no longer describes the program (item P1).
3. **Pre-rim, smeared-reference numbers still quoted as current (stale document).** `theory.md` 2.2 and `validation.md` 30.1 quoted 31 to 47 % (copper edge), 45 to 68 % (track) and 3 to 4 times high (via outside an edge): the program of 2.5.x against the program's own 3D solid, which smears the barrel. Since 2.6.0 the via outside the edge is uncoupled (effect lost, about 1.5 % of the rise, safe) and the rest reads low. Corrected (theory 2.2, validation 30.1, the README notes).
4. **The via near a cooled edge: theory 2.6 quoted numbers against a reference later found hot (wrong document).** 'FDM 4 to 10 % too small at 1 mm, 3D solid about 19 points too large at 1 mm' rest on the cell-centred reference that validation 46 found +2.0 / +8.5 % hot in the via effect; the finite elements give the FDM 2 to 20 % (eight sub-gaps) or 2.6 to 6.5 / 13 to 23 % (default, 200 / 1000 W/m2K) at 1 mm and the 3D solid +9.7 / +25.8 % at 0.5 mm, 1 mm not measured. Theory corrected; the same old numbers are in the live warning of `checks.check_via_limits` (item P1).
5. **The Shen case: '+0.7 %' was a cancellation and is stale (wrong document).** The program's hottest point (1.472 K/W) was compared with the solver's mean (1.461), with the barrel over `d`: two errors of about 9 % of opposite sign. Like for like on 2.7.1 the FVM is -1.5 %, the FDM -1.5 %, the 3D solid -4.3 %, the Lumped +4.1 %, the Analytic +1.2 %. Corrected in validation 18.2 and the via-contact-geometry README. The CFD 1.86 K/W stays not reproduced; the gap is in the paper's inputs (its no-via 120.3 K/W needs FR-4 0.35 W/mK against its Table I 0.29).
6. **The target radius statement was with the old barrel (stale document).** '-2.1 to +3.9 %' (theory 1.5) is the barrel over `d`; with the barrel of 2.6.0 the unit cells read -2.4 to +0.9 % (whole) and -0.9 to +1.1 % (drilled). A third configuration (the barrel the only path, plating resolved) puts the construction 0.9 to 9.2 % on the resistive (safe) side, with a best radius 0.92 to 1.0 `r_v`. The pair (`e^(-1/4)`, 0.5) is a constant between references (-6.5 to +9.2 % of the via resistance over the three), not a property of a via. Corrected in theory 1.5 and validation 34.1.
7. **Section 28 kept superseded paragraphs that read as results (misleading document).** The h_side 1000 row (+34.3 %), the 'deficit of 17 to 26 % that does not converge' and the strong-edge table against the hot reference are now marked superseded, with the finite-element numbers to quote.
8. **The Lumped and Analytic via accuracy rests on the program's own FVM (limit to record).** The table of validation 18.1 (29 + 17 boards) is against the FVM at 0.25 mm, measured before the barrel of 2.6.0 and the Lumped changes of 2.6.0 to 2.7.0, by scripts that are not in the repository; not re-run here. The independent numbers now added beside it: Lumped +11 to +31 % and Analytic -4.5 to +3.9 % next to a 200 W/m2K wall (-17 to -20 % at 1000), zoned cluster -2.4 / +1.0 % and -0.6 / -2.3 %, Shen +4.1 / +1.2 %. The implemented Lumped variant (cap 0.75 fin lengths) is the one its own folder measured worse on arrays (copper-fill gain mean 53 %, maximum 132 % against the program's FVM); not re-run against a resolved reference (item T5).
9. **The Analytic tile Green function is capped at 64 modes (limit, program item P3).** Raising the cap moves the via effect by about 1 % (1.3 % on the example board's copper-fill gain, -0.8 / +0.9 % on 200 x 150 mm arrays); the folder already said so; re-measured on 2.7.1.
10. **A full plane with a lone via directly under a 1 mm part** reads the via effect 13 % low in the FVM at 0.25 mm and 49 % at 1 mm against the committed reference (safe side): the discretisation of a lone via under a small part, recorded in 34.2.

## 8. Verdict

**GO for trusting the via chapter of the FVM**: its closed forms are exact, its well and barrel agree with three independent resolved references within -6.5 to +9.2 % of the via resistance, every partial-copper case read so far is on the safe side, and next to a cooled wall it reads the via effect at most 3.6 % too large at the default settings (finite elements, validation 49 and `research/independent-fem-extensions` 8.1; +28 % at 1 mm with eight sub-gaps at 1000 W/m2K, which the strong-edge warning covers). **Conditional for the FDM** (the copper corner depends on which resolved reference is right, P2) and **for the reduced models** (their accuracy figures rest on the program's FVM; the independent numbers now beside them are +11 to +31 % for the Lumped near a wall). The documents of this branch are corrected; the warning texts are A6 (P1).
