# Review S1: the studies that rest on a published source, checked against the printed page

Track S1 of the 2.8.0 fine-comb audit (the maintainer: 'review the studies; those based on articles must match the articles'). Program: `development` 5fc91430. Branch `docs/audit-s1`. Nothing in `pcbthermal/` or `tests/` is edited; corrections of transcribed numbers in study scripts (`research/*/scripts`) are listed one by one.

**Method.** For each study whose conclusion rests on a published article, book, thesis or datasheet: (1) every number the study says the source prints is compared with the page IMAGE of the copy in the local papers library (`papers/pdf`, read only; pages rendered with pypdfium2 to a scratch folder; numbers read from a plot are re-digitised by pixel, axis ticks located on the image); (2) the case definition (geometry, conductivities, films, which surface, which quantity); (3) the result re-run when light; (4) a verdict: MATCHES / MATCHES NARROWER / TRANSCRIBED WRONG / CASE DIFFERS / NOT REPRODUCED / CANNOT BE CHECKED. Cases already settled by review A1 (`docs/audit-a1`, `releases/2.8.0/audit/REVIEW_A1.md`: Muzychka 2003 cases 1, 2 and the cross, Seri Lee through `blocks.py`, Lienhard Example 8.1, Yovanovich 2003 Table 3, the Teertstra channel formula) are taken as done after a quick check and are not repeated.
