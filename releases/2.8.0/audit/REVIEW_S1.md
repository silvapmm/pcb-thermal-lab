# Review S1: the studies that rest on a published source, checked against the printed page

Track S1 of the 2.8.0 fine-comb audit (the maintainer: 'review the studies; those based on articles must match the articles'). Program: `development` 5fc91430. Branch `docs/audit-s1`. Nothing in `pcbthermal/` or `tests/` is edited; corrections of transcribed numbers in study scripts (`research/*/scripts`) are listed one by one.

**Method.** For each study whose conclusion rests on a published article, book, thesis or datasheet: (1) every number the study says the source prints is compared with the page IMAGE of the copy in the local papers library (`papers/pdf`, read only; pages rendered with pypdfium2 to a scratch folder; numbers read from a plot are re-digitised by pixel, axis ticks located on the image); (2) the case definition (geometry, conductivities, films, which surface, which quantity); (3) the result re-run when light; (4) a verdict: MATCHES / MATCHES NARROWER / TRANSCRIBED WRONG / CASE DIFFERS / NOT REPRODUCED / CANNOT BE CHECKED. Cases already settled by review A1 (`docs/audit-a1`, `releases/2.8.0/audit/REVIEW_A1.md`: Muzychka 2003 cases 1, 2 and the cross, Seri Lee through `blocks.py`, Lienhard Example 8.1, Yovanovich 2003 Table 3, the Teertstra channel formula) are taken as done after a quick check and are not repeated.

## 2. Summary

Rows by primary verdict: **MATCHES 18** (rows 2, 4, 5, 7 to 11, 13, 15 to 18, 22 to 24, and the CSD17308Q3 / TPS7A47 transcriptions of row 14), **MATCHES NARROWER 3** (3, 6, 21), **TRANSCRIBED WRONG 1** (1), **CASE DIFFERS 1 board in 2 places** (the JEDEC 2s2p board, rows 14 and 20), **NOT REPRODUCED, as the studies themselves say 3** (Shen's CFD 1.86 and 120.3 K/W; the Biot plateau of Culham and Yovanovich; the via resistances of Lee, Lemczyk and Yovanovich 1992), **CANNOT BE CHECKED 4 rows plus parts of 2** (12, 19, 25, JESD51-7; parts of 11 and 13).

What this changes:

1. `paper-case-studies` 13: one plot value of Lee and Yovanovich (1989) was misread (89.0 K for 79.5 K); the "R1 fails on the glass second peak" was an artefact. Script, README and figure 08 corrected. The program's +10.3 % on the glass plate is against the independent solver and does not move.
2. JEDEC 2s2p board (calculator row and the exposed-pad row of validation 29.2): the cited board source prints a 70 um backside plane and vias joined to layers 1, 2 and 4; the study draws neither. With the plane the model reads 26.6 instead of 31.6 K/W (the calculator 1.222 instead of 1.029 W for 1 W). The documents already flagged the reading as a text extraction; it is now confirmed on the page image. **Decision for the maintainer (and A5)**: obtain JESD51-7 and redraw the board, or keep the row as 'traces reading, source differs'.
3. The Goldstein-Lau constant 0.475 of the face down: the summary page states `L = A/P` only for the face up; A/P for the face down is an inference (theory 8 and the face-down README now say so). No number moves; whether Goldstein and Lau used A/P for the face down needs their paper (not in the library).
4. theory.md: section references of the glass plate fixed (13, not 12) and the conjugate solution named as the folder's solver; YMC 1999 Table 2 statement narrowed.
