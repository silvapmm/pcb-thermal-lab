# Final code-against-theory review of 2.8.0, part 1a: `docs/theory.md` chapters 0 to 4

Scope: the symbols (0), the physical description (1.1 to 1.8), the FVM (2), the FDM (3) and the 3D solid (4) of `docs/theory.md`, read against `pcbthermal/model.py` (`Board.face_film_h`, `slab_thicknesses`, `gap_thicknesses`), `geometry.py`, `laminate.py`, `viawell.py`, `copper.py`, `gerber/` (what 1.6 to 1.8 state), `fvm.py`, `fdm.py`, `solid3d.py`, `memory.py` and `checks.py` where the chapters quote a warning. Tree: `development` da16d80f (2.7.1 plus the merged 2.8.0 branches, the film term of `fix/face-film-half-slab` included). Branch `docs/final-review-1a`; documents only, nothing in `pcbthermal/` or `tests/` was edited. The audit files of `releases/2.8.0/audit/` (REVIEW_A1 to A5, L12, L1L5, L4L9, S1, DECISIONS 1 to 3) were the list of superseded values to look for.

## Method

For every section: the formula, constant and range stated was compared with the function that implements it (file and function below); one numeric example per section was recomputed by a light script (scratch, `PCBTHERMAL_CONFIG` private); the numbers quoted were compared with `docs/validation.md` and the audit reviews; every `validation.md` section and every figure the chapters cite was checked to exist; the stale values of the audit (13 to 17 % low, 98.075, 832 / 864 as current, 0.15 Ra^1/3, 'Planned change (decision 8)', 2244, '4 to 10 % at 1 mm', '19 points') were searched for. Runs: `tests/test_maximum_principle.py -s` (16 s) and `python -m pcbthermal solvers examples/example_board.pcbt.json --cell-mm 1` and `2`; nothing above a few seconds of solve, no heavy lock taken.

## Trust level per chapter

* **0 and 1.1 to 1.5: high.** Every constant and formula is the one in the code; the film term is described as implemented with its derivation, constants, size, direction and what is not modelled, and its numbers are those of validation 55. The source statuses agree with section 12 (`[Patankar1980]` M, `[Peaceman1978]` S, `[Krogh1919]` M with (1.8) an own derivation, `[TI-AN2020]` R reproduced to 0.06 %).
* **1.6 to 1.8: medium-high.** The constants match; the evidence is the repository's own measurements and real boards (stated), no vendor document; the memory figures of the reader rest on one machine and on comments of `raster.py` (stated in the text).
* **2 FVM: high** for the equations, solvers and memory law (re-run numbers equal); **medium** for the via at a copper corner and near a strong edge, where the program side is reproduced but the two independent references disagree by 25 to 39 % (REVIEW_A3 item 1, recorded in 2.6) and the live warnings still carry the withdrawn numbers (items 1 and 2).
* **3 FDM: medium.** The construction matches the code; its own via defect near a strong edge is a stated limit whose cause is not separated.
* **4 3D solid: medium-high.** The slab rule, the face and edge films and the cut-cell links match the code; the slab and cell error of a small part and the via on a cell line are stated limits; the via next to a strong edge reads too large (unsafe side, stated, 1 mm not measured).

## What was not verified

* The numbers of `validation.md` that the chapters quote were checked for consistency, not re-run, except the maximum-principle table and the SOR sweeps; the strong-edge, finite-element, laminate and memory-ladder numbers rest on the audit's runs (2.7.1) and the research folders.
* `papers/notes/SOURCE_MAP_AUDIT.md` is in the untracked local library; the statuses were checked against section 12 of `docs/theory.md` and `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` only.
* The spread numbers of 2.5 and the accuracy orders were not re-run after the film term (expected shift about 10⁻⁵ K, validation 55.2).
