# Final review of 2.8.0, part 2c: what the user reads

Scope: the HTML user guide (`pcbthermal/help/index.html`), the calculator help (`pcbthermal/help/calculator.html`), `docs/calculators.md`, `README.md`, the warning texts of `pcbthermal/checks.py`, `api.py`, `analytic.py`, `copper.py`, `profiles.py`, the tooltips of `gui/tips.py`, `gui/preferences.py` (`TIPS`, `LIBRARY_TIPS`) and `gui/calculator.py`, the About box (`gui/help.py`), `docs/versions.md` and the Unreleased section of `CHANGELOG.md`, read against the corrected theory and validation (parts 1a, 1b, 2a and 2b of this review) and the audit of `releases/2.8.0/audit/` (`REVIEW_A2`, `A3`, `A4`, `A5`, `L12`, `L1L5`, `DECISIONS_2` 15 to 28). Tree: `development` da16d80f; branch `docs/final-review-2c`. The audit's text branch (`fix/audit-texts`, step 4a of `DECISIONS_2` 31) was never made: this pass carries the text items of it that need no new measurement.

**Rule kept**: text only. No formula, threshold, constant or firing rule of the code changed; only the wording and the numbers that the warnings, the guide, the help and the tooltips quote. `docs/theory.md`, `docs/models.md` and `docs/validation.md` are not edited here (other parts hold them).
