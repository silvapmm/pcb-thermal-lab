# Review of `fix/via-and-edge-warning-texts`

**Verdict: GO** for the merge into `development`, after the corrections below (committed on the branch), with the whole battery left to the tester. Text only: no number of the program moves (`tests/regression` at 2 mm 153 passed).

## What the branch does

It adds to the warnings what the independent finite-element comparison of `research/independent-fem-extensions` 8.1 measured for vias next to a strongly cooled edge, with the wording of the reviewer's opinion `releases/2.8.0/opinions/2026-11-15-open-questions.md` sections 1, 2 and 4: a clause for the FDM and one for the 3D solid in the strong-edge warning of the FVM and FDM (`checks._edge_via_clause`, both the cut and the uncut-laminate forms), a clause for the Analytic in the reduced-model edge warning and in the one-or-two-vias warning, and the 3D solid numbers in the edge-via warning of `check_via_limits`; documents in `docs/validation.md` 49, `models.md`, `theory.md`, `versions.md`, the user guide and the CHANGELOG.

## Checks

1. **Every number against its source.** The program side of study 1 was re-run on this tree (open barrel; FDM with eight sub-gaps at 1 and 0.25 mm, Analytic at 0.5 mm, 3D solid at 0.5 and 0.25 mm; the six cases: via 0.16 and 3 mm from the wall and the 3 x 3 array, at 200 and 1000 W/m2K) against the finest finite-element E of README 8.1. Every error of the table came back to the printed digit (FDM 8: -2.3 / -2.3 / -0.8 and -8.6 / -4.3 / -4.0 % at 0.25 mm, -5.8 / -3.4 / -2.0 and -19.7 / -12.4 / -10.5 % at 1 mm; Analytic +3.9 / -4.4 / -2.8 and -20.1 / -20.2 / -17.0 %; 3D solid +9.7 / +1.9 / +0.6 and +25.8 / +13.8 / +11.1 % at 0.5 mm, +3.2 / +1.2 / +1.3 and +9.6 / +6.7 / +6.1 % at 0.25 mm; the -4.4 against the README's -4.5 is a rounding of the reference). The finite-element side was **read, not re-run** (the scratch environment with scikit-fem and pyamg is not installed; the folder's `out/` cache is not present); its own review (README 9.1) re-ran the 0.2 mm level to the digit. The ranges of the texts follow: FDM 4 to 9 % at 0.25 mm and 1000 W/m2K, up to 20 % at 1 mm; 3D solid up to about 26 % at 0.5 mm and 7 to 14 % for the via 3 mm away; Analytic 17 to 20 % at 1000, within 5 % at 200 W/m2K. The board (10 x 10 mm, four layers of 35 um, gaps 0.487 mm, so 1.6 mm, bottom film 200 W/m2K, every wall cooled) matches the text.
2. **Signs.** E is the hottest rise with the via copper off minus on (`study_hollow_barrel.py` line 11, the error `(b - v - ref) / ref`): a negative error is a via effect too small, the part hotter than the truth, the safe side (FDM, Analytic); a positive one the unsafe side (3D solid). The texts say so.
3. **Triggers.** The FDM clause appears only with `fdm` in the run and a via on the board, the 3D clause only with `solid3d` and the FVM or FDM (the trigger of the strong-edge warning: a 3D-only run has no edge-laminate warning, as validation 49.1 says), the Analytic clause only with the Analytic and a via (not for the Lumped alone). Tested.
4. **Live check and the warning box.** The clauses are string concatenation inside `check_model_limits`; `tests/test_live_check_speed.py` passed. The warnings were rendered offscreen in the `WarningAccordion` for a 30 x 20 mm four-layer board with an edge of 1000 W/m2K, a via inside and one at the edge, and looked at: the closed rows keep their short titles (the first clause, 'Edge coefficient above 200 W/m2K: the edge laminate is…'), the opened row wraps the long text in a 380 px dock.

## Findings and corrections

1. **Defect in the text (fixed):** the one-line form of both strong-edge warnings read '... at a coarse cell.; FDM via effect ...' (the clause was appended after the final full stop). The short texts now end with the clauses and one full stop; a test holds it for the cut and the uncut forms.
2. **Imprecise claim (fixed):** 'The FDM also reads the effect of the vias low away from the edge (4 to 9 % ...)' read as if the deficit were only far from the edge, and did not say that the 4 to 9 % is the FDM's own part with eight sub-gaps, beyond the laminate error that the same warning already states (with the default two sub-gaps the FDM reads 9 to 16 % low at 0.25 mm and 1000 W/m2K, the FVM itself -7 to -13 %). The text now says 'on its own, beyond the laminate error above, next to the edge and away from it' and that the part reads hotter; the docstring, `models.md` and validation 49.1 say the same, and '1 to 3 points' became '1 to 3 %' (the README's 'points' are points below the FVM, the numbers are percent of E).
3. **Missing test (added):** the short form of the 3D clause, the 3D solid beside the FVM without the FDM, a 3D-only run, and the uncut-laminate form.
4. **Documents asked by the opinion and missing (added):** the FDM limit in `docs/theory.md` 3 (opinion section 1) and the new 3D numbers in the Planned bullet 'Board-edge image of the via in the FDM and the 3D solid' (opinion section 4); validation 49.1 records the re-run.
5. **Section number:** 49 is the next after 48; no other branch or worktree adds a section 49 (`git log --all -S'## 49.'`, the open worktrees).

## Limits, stated

The numbers are one 10 x 10 mm board with every wall cooled ('3 mm from the wall' is still close to a wall on it): a sample, not a bound, as the texts say. The 3D solid's 'about halves with each halving of the cell' rests on two cells (0.5 and 0.25 mm; the ratio is 0.33 to 0.55). The via test is `problem.vias` non-empty, so a via with no board under it (ignored by every model) still adds the clause; harmless. The FDM edge-via warning keeps its older '4 to 10 % too small at 1 mm' (an insulated edge); next to a 1000 W/m2K wall the FDM with eight sub-gaps reads up to 20 % at 1 mm, which the strong-edge clause now says.
