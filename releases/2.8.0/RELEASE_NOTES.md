# PCB Thermal Lab 2.8.0

A version of confidence: measured, bounded and warned errors.

## Highlights

* **A finite-element model as a cross-check** (steady state; key `fem`, off by default, pyamg optional): a structurally independent method beside the FVM, the LPTN, the Analytic, the FDM and the 3D solid.
* **The surface read-out beside the column mean**: for a thick copper layer the hottest surface value is always computed and shown next to the column mean, each named by its read-out.
* **A rebuilt Simulation panel and clearer warnings**: grouped settings, one name per model, run feedback in the status bar, a Warnings list that says what to do.

## What to know when upgrading

* Some numbers move on purpose (`releases/2.8.0/WHAT_MOVED.md`): the FVM, FDM and Analytic by 1e-5 K typically and at most +0.13 K on the frozen boards, except where a drawn copper edge cuts a cell at a coarse cell (up to several K at 2 mm); a thick plate +0.93 K on the checked case; the LPTN part rows up to about 10 percent hotter (the part's own peak, the safe side); the 3D solid is unchanged.
* A 2.7.1 project edited in the Board panel may have had its copper thickness clipped at 210 um on that edit; 2.8.0 no longer does this and shows the stored value (it cannot know the old one).
* The FEM interface pad of a metal block carries no film on its side walls and conducts across only (10^-3 W/mK in its plane), as in the 3D solid; found by the final opinion against the 3D solid.
* Project files are unchanged in format. A project saved with the FEM ticked opens in 2.7.1 without it.

## Known limits

* The FEM is steady only: no Animation or Power profile run (its transient is planned for 2.9.0).
* The heavy studies of the reproduction pass were not run (by decision); `docs/version-comparison.md` is not regenerated and says so.

* **Additions, nothing moves**: 29 FEM records (column 0.0 to 2.7 % of the rise below the FVM at 1 mm on the frozen boards) (the finned block FEM records regenerated after the interface-pad correction: +0.29 to +0.30 K; the other FEM records did not move) and the surface metrics of 102 FVM and FDM records (1.4 mK above the column, median, on 35 um copper).
