# Review: the simple stack-up takes the physical layer slabs

Branch: `fix/simple-stackup-physical-layers` (20ff27e3, 95e1a90e; cut from `develop` fa6eecb4, before the drawn-layer centroid film and the FDM harmonic face mean; `develop` ca51c593 merged in by the reviewer, 81f49b43). Reviewer's verdict: **GO**, with the corrections below made on the branch, the limits recorded in `docs/validation.md` 42.6 and one open question for the maintainer.

## Verdict in short

* The code does what `docs/theory.md` 1.2.1 says: `Board.slab_thicknesses()` is the copper plus half of each adjacent gap in both stack-ups, a one-layer board keeps the whole thickness. One-layer and two-layer boards with equal copper give the same slabs and bit-identical fields in the FVM, FDM, Lumped and Analytic (checked: `np.array_equal` on a 40 x 30 mm board with a pad and `h_side` 200). The simple stack-up and the advanced one with equal gaps are bit-identical (the branch's test passes).
* The claims reproduce on the merged tree: frozen numbers at 2, 1 and 0.5 mm 317 passed with the regenerated pending file; the branch's and the touched tests (`test_simple_stackup_physical`, `test_analytic_edge`, `test_profiles`, `test_stackup`, `test_via_edge_image`, `test_laminate_edge_band`, `test_fdm_harmonic_face`, `test_imported_edge_film_centroid`) 161 passed. The records of the pending file that the merge did not touch are equal to the executor's to the last digit.
* Against an independent 3D solver with a cooled side wall (`scripts/study_simple_stackup_review.py`, isotropic FR-4, 20 x 20 mm, same plan cell 0.5 mm): of 39 boards (4 and 6 layers, equal, lighter and heavier inner copper, full planes, a top pad over the inner planes, pads on the outer layers only, `h_side` none, 50 and 1000 W/m2K) the new slabs are closer or equal in 33 for the FVM, 33 for the FDM and 28 for the Lumped. Largest gains: pads on the outer layers, FVM -4.13 to -1.99 % (insulated) and -5.27 to -2.54 % (1000 W/m2K), FDM -9.17 to -7.30 %; full planes at 1000 W/m2K -13.84 to -13.00 %. Worse: the top pad over inner planes at 1000 W/m2K (FVM 0.4 to 2.9 points, finding 3), and at most 0.04 point elsewhere (heavy inner copper with full planes at 50 W/m2K; the Lumped +0.01 to +0.02 point on full copper insulated).

## Other checks

* **Metal block** (example board with a 3 mm aluminium block on the bottom, 2 mm cell): FVM 82.125 to 82.213 °C, FDM 81.338 to 81.422, Lumped 78.261 to 78.321, Analytic 81.557 to 81.634; the 3D solid 83.784 unchanged (it reads the gaps directly, so it does not move on any board; the layered models move towards it). The half-slab resistance of the block contact (`blocks.py`) follows the new outer slab.
* **Unequal copper on two layers** (70 / 35 um, full copper): moves, as it should (69.0987 to 69.0940 °C); the claim 'two-layer boards unchanged' holds only for equal copper, as the documents say.
* **Analytic edge parameter**: `beta = h_side Σ slab / Σ S` is unchanged for the simple stack-up (Σ slab is the thickness, Σ S the same laminate and copper in total); only the per-layer `beta_l` of the per-layer edge moves.
* **Transient and profiles**: the outer node holds 0.278 instead of 0.4 mm on the four-layer board, so its capacity falls and the node swing of a fast wave rises (0.393 to 0.543 K in `test_profiles`); the decision takes the closed-form floor of `stackimpedance` (2.013 K, it reads the gaps, not the slabs), so the regime does not change; times to 63 % of the three moved boards are in the pending file.
* **Memory guard and 3D solid**: unchanged (the unknown count depends on the gaps and the laminate rule, not on the slabs; the 3D solid builds its slabs from the gaps).
* **Project files**: no slab is stored (`project.py`, `fileschema.py`); an older project opens with the same stack-up and gives the new numbers. The user guide and the tooltips describe the total thickness shared by copper and gaps, which stays true.
* **Not run**: the whole battery (the maintainer's), a transient against an independent solver, imported boards of three or more layers with a simple stack-up (none in the harness; they move like drawn ones).

## Open decision for the maintainer

The edge warning does not fire when the laminate rule cuts no gap (thin gaps: six layers or more on 1.6 mm), while a strongly cooled edge there reads about 13 % cool at 0.25 mm against a reference that is itself still moving with the cell. Extend the warning to thin gaps (text only), or first converge the reference (finer plan cell near the wall) to know the true size.
