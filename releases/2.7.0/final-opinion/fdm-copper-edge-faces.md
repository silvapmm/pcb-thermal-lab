# Review: the FDM face of a drawn copper layer from the copper fill of the cells

Branch: `fix/fdm-copper-edge-faces` (556e47db, from `develop` 6e66ae05; `develop` 4d98d71b merged in by the reviewer, the branch's validation section renumbered 46 -> 47 because 46 is the finite-element check of `fix/edge-warning-fem-numbers`). Reviewer's verdict: **GO**, with the document corrections and the one test below (code unchanged).

## Verdict in short

* The code does what `docs/theory.md` 3 says: `fdm.Network._copper_face_sheets` builds `S_face = (A_face + B f_face) c_face` from the fill of the two cells the face runs through (`A` recovered from the nodal sheet after the laminate rule, so the sub-gaps a node keeps are respected), only on drawn copper layers, only where a nodal fill differs from the face fill by more than 1e-9, only with the laminate rule, area sampling and the harmonic mean. The face value is shared by the two nodes, so the matrix stays symmetric; `np.maximum(.., 0)` keeps every link non-negative; capacities, vertical links, films, via sheets (`via_S`, the nodal fill) and sources are untouched, so the unknowns, the pattern (example board at 0.5 mm: 1 077 251 entries both ways) and the memory guard are unchanged; network build 0.05 s, solve 1.14 -> 1.21 s on the example board at 0.5 mm.
* Every number the branch quotes that the reviewer re-ran reproduces to the digit: the strong-edge plate with the copper 0.5 mm in, against the finite-element limits (`tmp/fem/REPORT.md` 3(a)), FDM harmonic -> cell-fill at 0.5 mm -1.26 / -5.09 / -12.21 -> -0.95 / -2.28 / -4.45 % (10 / 200 / 1000 W/m2K), at 0.25 mm -0.97 / -3.47 / -7.65 -> -0.56 / -1.48 / -2.97 %; copper to the wall unchanged (-1.44 / -6.35 % at 0.5 mm, bit-identical); `plate_pad` -7.31 / -4.32 -> -5.36 / -2.86 % and `one_layer_pad` -6.75 / -4.07 -> -5.20 / -2.75 % against `tests/helpers/layered3d.py`; the aligned square (whole-box board) at 200 / 1000 W/m2K -7.33 / -13.91 -> -5.30 / -9.43 % at 1 mm; the strips board -33.63 / -23.43 / -15.89 -> -23.05 / -15.00 / -10.37 %.
* Frozen numbers at 2, 1 and 0.5 mm: 317 passed with the pending file (peak 3.4 GB); without it, at 2 mm, exactly its 7 steady records and 7 times fail. The four pending files on the merged tree hold no record or time twice (checked by key).
* Invariants re-run: matrix symmetric (asymmetry 0), no positive off-diagonal beyond the existing via couplings (96 entries of 1.2e-6 W/K on a via board, identical with the switch off), row sums equal to the ambient conductance to 1e-12 relative on imported, hatched, via and T-outline boards.
* Imported copper (fill, lambda and faces mappings, edge at 200 W/m2K): bit-identical with the switch off; a test now holds it (`test_imported_copper_layers_keep_their_own_faces`, it was claimed but not tested).
* Profiles: a 30 % square wave on `plate_pad` at 0.02, 0.1, 1 and 20 Hz through `simulate` (FDM, 1 mm): the same regime in each (resolved, then average + ripple), the steady maximum 335.96 -> 342.51 degC (+2.0 % of the rise), the oscillation of the decision unchanged at 0.1 to 20 Hz (the closed-form floor).

## Findings

1. **Wrong claim, corrected (study / document).** 'Every copper-edge board gets closer and none overshoots' holds for the boards of the table, not for copper that reaches a turned or curved outline. Re-run against the finite elements (ten turned and shifted squares, the disc and the T of section 46, heater mean): the copper to the wall moves 0.05 to 0.5 point AWAY: square at 1000 W/m2K, 1 mm, -4.36 -> -4.76 % (worst -5.36 -> -5.70), disc to the rim -4.88 / -5.16 -> -5.33 / -5.58 %, T at 200 W/m2K -0.73 -> -1.20 % (0.5 mm -0.44 -> -0.61). Explanation tested, not assumed: with insulated walls the FDM is within 0.7 % (section 46 review), so the cooling is the FDM wall term; the weak harmonic link at the cut cells had offset it, and a variant that gives the cut cells even more copper (cell fill divided by the board share of the cell) reads further off (T -2.10 %, square -5.04 %). Two errors no longer cancelling; recorded in `validation.md` 47.1, `docs/versions.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`; not a reason for a no-go (0.5 point at most, the inset cases gain 1 to 5 points).
2. **Turned squares re-run (were quoted from the study).** Copper 1.2 mm in, mean (worst) of ten, 1 mm: 200 W/m2K -6.97 (-8.60) -> -5.77 (-8.10) %, 1000 W/m2K -14.23 (-17.57) -> -11.99 (-16.75) %; 0.5 mm -4.89 -> -3.77 % and -9.12 -> -6.86 %; disc 1.2 mm in at 1000 W/m2K, 1 mm -16.53 / -16.23 -> -14.49 / -13.79 %. The -5.8 / -12.0 % of the branch are confirmed; the gain is mostly at the aligned angle (the cut-cell mixing dominates the turned ones).
3. **Unexplained FDM result, not caused by this branch.** The same 20 mm square reads differently in the FDM as the whole board box or as an outline in a 32 mm box: 200 W/m2K, 1 mm, -7.33 against -5.03 % (harmonic), -5.30 against -2.44 % (cell fill); 1000 W/m2K -13.91 against -9.14 %; the FVM is identical in both (-1.30, -2.78 %). Insulated, the two FDM boards agree (139.69 / 139.72 K); the gap grows with `h_side` (10: 123.22 / 123.35 K, 200: 53.01 / 54.33 K), so the FDM edge film on the board box and on an outline side differ. For the maintainer: an FDM item (the 'aligned square' row of 47.1 and the turned squares are therefore not the same FDM board).
4. **Strips row not reconciled (document).** The study's strips board puts the 4 x 4 mm part at y 8 to 12 mm over a strip AND a gap (the text said 'on a strip'); the strips row of section 41 is another board (its FVM column differs too: -8.99 against -11.77 % at 1 mm), whose geometry is in no script; a part wholly on a strip (4 x 2 / 2 x 2 mm) gives FVM -5.01 / -4.84 %, not -8.99. Stated in 47.2; the section 41 row is a number no script reproduces.
5. **Stale pointer (document).** The update note of section 41 pointed to section 46; now 47. Pointers in `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/models.md`, `docs/theory.md`, `docs/versions.md` and the docstring of `fdm.Network._copper_face_sheets` follow the renumbering.
6. **Warnings quoting the FDM at a copper edge** (`check_small_part_laminate` over a pad, `check_via_partial_copper`, the user guide): not re-measured. The new face moves the FDM toward the references on every inset and pad board measured, so the low-side ranges become upper bounds; for the via warning one board was checked (a top pad over a full plane, four vias: via effect at 1 mm 4.94 -> 5.33 K against 6.66 K of the FVM at 0.25 mm, same side). The 'up to 11 % too hot' side of the via warning was not checked for the FDM. No text changed.
