# Version history

What was implemented in every version (with the module where it lives and how it is tested) and what we still want to implement (last chapter, "Planned"). The release notes are `CHANGELOG.md`; the physics is in `models.md` and `theory.md`, the numbers in `validation.md`. **Newest first.** Dates are those of the release tags. Each bullet reads: feature (code; tests).

---

## 2.4.5 (in `develop`, not released): a part across the board outline

* **`theory.md` rewritten for the models as implemented** (`docs/theory-rewrite`; evidence `research/theory`). Each model section has one structure (the idea in a picture, unknowns, equations, assembly, solution, limits in words, where it is checked, sources) and the sources are cited by key with their reading status (section 12). Symbols were renamed where one letter had two meanings (`G_mod`, `C_loc` for the Analytic correction `(I + G_mod C_loc) y = y₀`, `N_ring`, `κ` for the wavenumber, `R_c`, `ϕ_ch`, `Θ_part`, ...). The history that the old text carried is here, not in the theory: the weight of a part across the outline was the coverage of the part alone before 2.4.5, then the intersection normalised to the board cover sampled 4 x 4 (`fix/part-across-outline`), and is the plain intersection of an 8 x 8 cover since `fix/board-cover-8x8`; the SOR relaxation factor was `2/(1 + sin(pi/n_max))` before `fix/sor-omega`; the via was one point conductance in the cell of its centre before `fix/point-via`; the Lumped and Analytic models joined the planes through the barrel conductance alone before `fix/via-constriction`; the fixed-point loop of the top path was replaced by a root (`fix/top-path-convergence`); the side walls of a finned block carried the fin ratio before `fix/finned-block-sides`; the limit of the 3D solid was 2 million unknowns before `fix/gui-compute-hang`; a one-layer board had two laminate nodes in every cell before `fix/laminate-nodes` (the Lumped model and the boards with a block still have them). The numbers that the theory quoted are in `validation.md` 25.
* **One factorisation for the top-path split** (`fix/top-path-speed`, 2.4.5; speed only, bit-identical). `fvm.factor_reuse()` (a context, one LU per thread, released when the outermost block ends) and `fvm.direct_solve(A, b)` (spsolve outside the block; inside it `splu` keyed by a hash of the CSC structure and values, the old LU released before a new one is made); `fvm.Network.solve_steady` and `fdm.Network.solve_steady` call it, and `api._solve_with_tc` opens the block around the power-coefficient loop and the top-path split. With fixed face coefficients all the board solves of a run share one LU (8 mm MOSFET pad, FVM 0.5 mm: 6 to 1 factorisations, 4.9 to 0.7 s; two top parts with a power coefficient on the example board at 1 mm: 42 to 1, 15.4 to 1.4 s); with computed coefficients the matrix changes at every Picard step and nothing is reused. Tests: `tests/test_top_path.py` (one factorisation, same temperatures as a fresh factorisation to 1e-9 K, LU released; a matrix that differs by 1e-12 is factorised again).
* **Laminate nodes for patterned copper** (`fix/laminate-nodes`, 2.4.5; evidence `research/laminate-nodes`, `research/bare-spot-under-source`, `research/zone-union`; theory `theory.md` 1.4; validation `validation.md` 21.2). `laminate.py`: a gap between two copper nodes is cut into `n = min(4, ceil(d / 0.4 mm))` sub-gaps in the FVM and FDM cells where the copper fill of either adjacent layer is below 1 plus a margin of `ceil(d/dx) + 1` cells (Euclidean distance); every other cell keeps the single link, so a board with copper in every cell is the earlier model bit for bit (except the one-layer board, whose two laminate nodes are now this rule down to a bottom node with the bottom film). The path through the gap is lengthened by the laminate that replaces absent copper, and the face value of a part adds `Q t_cu/(2 k_z A)` over bare laminate (peak and bare share of the mean). `fvm.Network.solve_sor` relaxes each plan column as a block (red-black point SOR has no valid colouring where cut and uncut columns meet with even `n`). On a thick simple 3-layer stack-up the inner node holds only its laminate. The FVM against the resolved 3D reference: 2-layer window 6 mm at 1.6 mm -18.44 to -3.07 %, at 3.2 mm -34.19 to -9.13 %, 1-layer pad 10 mm -4.08 to -1.80 %. The number of unknowns grows (2 layers at 0.5 mm 64 000 to 160 000; about 7 GB per million), so the "slow" note of the solver panel starts at 500 000 unknowns and says up to 7 GB per million. The direct solve grows faster than the unknowns (the LU fill grows with the square of the node layers): a 60 x 60 mm 2-layer board with an 8 mm pad at 0.5 mm, bare laminate almost everywhere, has 71 808 unknowns instead of 28 800, 14.9 instead of 2.2 million entries in the LU and 0.76 instead of 0.054 s of CPU per solve; the example board at 0.5 mm 1.8 instead of 1.1 s; the top-path study of `research/component-heatsink` (about 30 board solves per row with computed h) went from about 4 s to 30 to 45 s of CPU per row (the node prototype of the row left out). The CSD17308Q3 row of the Power Calculator moves from 0.983 to 0.968 W (`calculators.md`). The input warning of a part on bare laminate quotes the new ranges. Tests: `test_laminate.py`, `test_laminate_rule.py`, `test_layered_vs_3d.py`, `test_unknown_count.py`, `test_top_path.py`, `test_profiles.py`, `test_part_across_outline.py`, `test_fvm.py`. Not done (Planned): Lumped and Analytic, boards with a metal block, the 3D solid (reports the centre of its top slab).
* **Constriction of the heat converging on a via in the Lumped and Analytic models** (`fix/via-constriction`, 2.4.5; evidence `research/lumped-analytic-vias`, `research/lumped-via-groups`, `research/young-omega-tiles`; theory `theory.md` 1.5, 5.1 and 6.1). Both models carry an area mean (a ring, a tile) while the barrel sees its wall, and joined the planes through the barrel conductance alone, so the via effect was overstated by 35 to 60 % (copper-fill gain of the example board 6.3 to 6.8 K against 4.2 K of the FVM). `pcbthermal/viaconstriction.py`: `krogh` (the cell of a sink disc, target radius `a = e^(-1/4) r_via`), `ViaSet` (nearest neighbours, groups by single linkage at twice the larger nearest-neighbour distance), `ring_couplings` (Lumped: a well `R_K(S, r_c, a) + N_g R_K(S, R_macro, r_cl)` per plane and ring, barrel nodes eliminated by Schur complement, couplings between all planes, matrix size unchanged, no vias = unchanged), and `plan`, `spread_weights`, `add_self_terms` (Analytic: tile `min(1 mm, 0.75 median d_nn)`, at least 0.25 mm, a lone via shared bilinearly among four tiles, self-term on the diagonal of `G`; the active tiles of vias, heatsinks and blocks times the layers are kept to 4800 unknowns, 0.9 GB and 3 s at that size). Copper-fill gain of the example board: FVM 4.19, Lumped 4.48, Analytic 4.27 at 0.5 mm, 3D solid 4.49 at 1 mm. Error of the via effect against the FVM at 0.25 mm on the 29 study boards: Analytic mean 20 -> 1.8 % (max 66 -> 16 %), Lumped mean 33 -> 8 % (max 104 -> 22 %); on 17 boards not used to build it Analytic 2.9 % mean (-14 to +8 %), Lumped 20 % mean (-21 to +80 %), the program before 23 % and 31 %. The Lumped is accepted as an average improvement and is warned for what it does not resolve (a lone via -22 to +65 %, an array that straddles the edge of a footprint up to +80 %, round boards and a two-layer 80 x 60 mm board 16 to 21 % cool); the Analytic warns for the coarsened tile, a pitch under 0.45 mm, more than 2400 unknowns and one or two vias (up to 16 % too small). The API clamps the cell to 0.5 to 1 mm for these two models, so a 2 mm cell equals 1 mm. Tests `tests/test_via_constriction.py`; the zonal Lumped model (option C) and the limits stay in "Planned".
* **SOR relaxation factor** (`fix/sor-omega`, 2.4.5; speed only, same temperatures; evidence `research/young-omega-tiles`, `research/sor-with-via-couplings`). `fvm.Network.sor_omega`: Rayleigh-quotient estimate of Young's optimum on the full `K`, restricted to the linked nodes, `omega = 2/(1 + sqrt(1 - rho^2))` capped at 1.999 (a lower bound of the Jacobi radius, so on the safe side); the earlier `2/(1 + sin(pi/n))` is the optimum of fixed edges. Sweeps: example board 2244 -> 810 at 1 mm and 1145 -> 404 at 2 mm, 2.1 to 4.8 times fewer on the five boards of `docs/validation.md` 5; a board of 1 x 1 cell no longer diverges. Tests: `tests/test_sor_omega.py`. Limit: with uneven losses or partial copper the estimate stays 1.1 to 2 times above the sweeps of the best omega (the bound is loose there).
* **Point via and block SOR** (`fix/point-via`; evidence `research/point-via-convergence`, `research/sor-with-via-couplings`). `viawell.py`: the footprint of a via (area of its disc over the cells or nodes on the board, enlarged until its lattice effective radius is `0.78 r_v`; the potential kernel of the five-point stencil), the Peaceman well, the Kron reduction of the barrel nodes and the matrix entries `G_lm (e_l w - e_m w)(e_l w - e_m w)^T`; `fvm.Network` (`via_blocks`, `gz_via`, the Lumped reads `gz + gz_via`), `fdm.Network` (footprint of nodes) and `solid3d.Solid` (one footprint rule for open and filled vias) use it. `fvm.Network.solve_sor` is a block SOR (blocks of the footprints coloured greedily, exact block solve relaxed by omega; `fvm.MAX_SOR_BLOCK = 5000`, above it the direct solver and `info["fallback"]`, `extras["fallback_direct"]` and a warning in the result). `checks.check_via_limits` (a via within one cell of the edge; a cell of 2 mm or more). Documents: `docs/theory.md` ("The via on the grid"), `docs/models.md`, `docs/validation.md` 1, 2, 2.1 and 5. A via with no board under its drill disc (`geometry.discs_board_share`: centre plus 6 rings of 24 samples; `Problem.via_board_shares`, `drop_off_board_vias`) is ignored by every model (FVM, FDM, 3D solid, Lumped and Analytic, also in their transients and profiles) instead of being moved to the nearest board cell; the 3D solid's point fallback is the part of the disc on the board; two input warnings (`checks.check_problem`); the live check is 0.19 s with 2000 vias and a 400-vertex outline (7.9 s before). Tests: `tests/test_via_off_outline.py`, `tests/test_point_via.py` (gain of the 3 x 3 board against the cell, energy balance and zero row sum, FDM, Lumped, SOR equal to the direct solver, fallback, footprint on the board, dy not equal to dx, 3D solid rule, the two warnings).
* **Warnings of the limits of the models** (`fix/model-limit-warnings`, text only). `checks.check_model_limits(problem, models, cell_mm)`, called by `api.simulate` after `check_problem`: a part on bare laminate for the layered models (smallest fill in the central half of the footprint below 0.5; `research/bare-spot-under-source` P3), and for the Lumped model the vias under a part and one aggregated message for the vias outside every footprint (`research/lumped-analytic-vias` 4.3, `research/lumped-via-groups` 4.2). In `analytic._effective_sheet` the copper-spread trigger is the larger of two spreads, the second with the fin length of the layer as full copper (`research/analytic-partial-copper` 9.4: 70 warned, 49 silent of 119 boards, none of the silent beyond 5 %). Numbers in `validation.md` section 24. Tests: `tests/test_model_limit_warnings.py`. The coarse-cell via warning stays in "Planned" with `fix/point-via`, and so does wiring these checks into the live `check_problem` of the application (they run after Run, in the summary).
* **Part across the outline** (`fix/part-across-outline`, P0 of `research/rotated-square-3d`). A part that crosses the board outline gave each cut cell the power density of the whole cell (FVM, FDM, 3D solid). `geometry.part_weights(poly, outline, nx, ny, dx, dy, samples=8, cover=...)` is now the share of the board samples of the cell that lie in the part times the board cover the model uses for the cell (the FVM passes `Network.cover`, the 3D solid `bcov`; the cover is sampled 8 x 8 like the part, see the next item); without an outline it is `coverage_fraction`, bit-identical. `geometry._sample_mask(clip=)` returns the part mask and the outline mask on the same points; the 3D solid samples footprints 8 x 8 like the FVM; the input check "x % of the footprint is outside the board" uses `part_weights(..., 4)`. Hottest point of the example board cut to a T, FVM at 1 / 2/3 / 0.5 / 1/3 mm: 107.56 / 106.86 / 107.38 / 106.96 degC (before 109.06 / 106.86 / 110.80 / 106.96); edge-shift scatter of a small T at 1 mm 15.7 to 2.95 K (FVM) (`validation.md` section 23). Residual 0.5 to 0.7 K (about 1 % of the rise) at 1 to 0.5 mm: the cut cell conducts with its cover (the cut cells option stays in "Planned").
* **Board cover sampled 8 x 8** (`fix/board-cover-8x8`, after `fix/part-across-outline`). One constant, `geometry.BOARD_SAMPLES = 8`, sets the lattice of `Board.cover` (FVM, 3D solid), `part_weights` and the FDM node cover (the cover was 4 x 4 and the heaters 8 x 8), so the weight of a cut cell is exactly the intersection `n_pb / 64`. `coverage_fraction` samples a large polygon (a board outline) in full only in the cells an edge touches (`_coverage_edge_cells`, bit-identical, 30 to 60 times cheaper on fine grids). Edge-shift scatter of the small T at 1 mm: FVM 2.95 to 1.47 K, 3D solid 3.23 to 1.56 K; hottest point of the example T at 0.5 mm 107.38 to 107.37 (FVM) and 107.20 to 107.18 (3D solid). Smoother, not more accurate (mean bias +1.4 K at 1 mm remains, some edge positions up to 1 K worse). Tests: `tests/test_geometry.py`, `tests/test_part_across_outline.py`, `tests/test_outline.py`.
* **Read-outs on the board only** (`analysis._footprint_stats`, `_ring_mean`, `heater_stats`, `zone_stats` with `_board_mask`): the mean, minimum and ring temperatures under parts and zones leave out the cells or nodes off the board, which read the ambient (a zone on a T board showed a minimum of 25 degC; the mean under a small T part at 0.5 mm FVM 191.3 became 223.4 degC, the ring of `psi_jb` 163.1 became 203.7, the zone mean 128.8 became 180.5; the maximum and the junction temperature do not move); also the mean and ring points of the Power Calculator (`inverse.py`).
* Lumped and Analytic (rectangle of the same area) and boards without an outline are bit-identical (the tests store 217.73005348656147 and 237.28294907393308 degC).
* Tests: `tests/test_part_across_outline.py`.

## 2.4.4 - 2026-10-29: interface leftovers and the close-fin warning

* The Simulation footer (Run, progress, warnings) no longer takes a low dock: the settings keep at least 120 px (they scroll); the warning list is no longer of fixed height (a fixed height is also a minimum, so 30 warnings raised the minimum height of the window from 418 to 574 px) but shrinks down to `WarningList.MIN_HEIGHT` (40 px, two lines) and scrolls, and gets at most a quarter of the dock and 150 px (`panels.WarningList._fit`, `set_max_height`, `main_window._FooterLimiter`). Measured offscreen: the lowest window is 450 px without warnings (418 before: the settings strip went from 88 to 120 px) and 496 px with 30 warnings (574 before); a window cannot be made 420 px high.
* The count of unknowns under the cell size is a full-width row (it spans both columns of the form), so its wrapped text is no longer cut to one line; the cut was seen in a real window and is not reproduced offscreen, where the old row was not cut either, so the test checks the spanning row.
* A decimal comma pasted through the right-click menu of a number field is read as a point (`theme._CommaToPoint._context_menu`; the key and shortcut paths already did this). Qt builds the spin-box menu inside C++, so the menu is rebuilt with the same entries (Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete, Select All of the number without its unit, Step up, Step down) and a Paste that turns commas into points; the right click on the arrows (an event to the spin box itself) gets it too.
* A click on a warning between an edit and the next check does nothing: the list is marked stale at the edit (`WarningList.set_stale`) and refreshed by the check; a click on a removed element was already ignored.
* Tests: `tests/test_ui_cleanup.py`.
* **Close-fin warning on the channel Rayleigh number** (`checks.check_fin_channels`, `fin_channel_rayleigh`, `fin_channel_fraction`; called from `api._part_warnings` next to `check_thick_plates`). It replaces the input check "air gap under 4 mm in still air". After the run, for every finned block in still air: `theta` = mean excess of the block over the ambient air (`arrays["block_T"]` of the FVM, FDM or 3D solid over the cells or nodes inside the block polygon, weighted by `block_cov` so that the part of a block cut away by the board outline does not count; with the Lumped or Analytic the same quiet auxiliary FVM run as the thick-plate check, `api._block_flux_result`), `b` = pitch minus fin thickness, `L` = longer side of the bounding box of the block (the program has no fin direction), air properties at ambient + theta/2 (`convection.air_properties`), beta = 1/T_film, `Ra_b = g beta theta b^4 Pr / (nu^2 L)`; it warns when `Ra_b < 90` (`phi` = [1 + (14.16/Ra_b^(3/4))^2]^(-1/2) < 0.9) with `Ra_b`, `phi`, the loss and the statement that it is for a vertical board. Of the 667 cases of the 1080-case grid of the study that lose 10 % or more, `Ra_b < 90` misses 1, the old rule 367. Forced air is not checked. No result changes (30 runs bit-identical to 2.4.3: five models, gaps 1.5 to 14.5 mm). Tests: `tests/test_fin_channel_warning.py`, `tests/test_heatsink_part.py`. Evidence: `research/fin-channel/README.md` 6 and `INTEGRATION.md` 2. The text depends on the board orientation and the face of the block, and gives the loss as an upper bound below `Ra_b` 14 (`FIN_CHANNEL_RA_BOUND`).

## 2.4.3 - 2026-10-29: corrections from the investigations

* **3D solid: a via larger than its cell keeps its copper**: when the copper-equivalent area of a via exceeds the area of a cell (a copper-filled 0.5 mm via at 0.25 mm cells) it is spread over the cells of its disc in proportion to the part each covers, and the barrel area is conserved (it kept 32 %); a warning is logged if several vias still fill a cell beyond 100 % (`solid3d.Solid.via_frac`, `tests/test_solid3d_via_spread.py`). Smaller vias are unchanged.

* **Zone union per sample point**: zones of one layer are combined by the union of their points, then averaged over the cell (`geometry.zone_fill`, used by the FVM, FDM, 3D solid, Analytic and the input check), instead of the larger of their shares of a cell; two zones that meet inside a cell no longer lose the copper of one of them (0.6 to 0.7 K on a 20 K rise in the test case; real copper 5 to 15 % cool before). The example boards give the same numbers (`tests/test_zone_union.py`). The input check also warns of a zone whose outline crosses itself (`geometry.polygon_self_intersects`), and a test keeps the per-node maximum of the FDM point sampling.
* **Heat source with component fields**: the input check warns that a pure source ignores `theta`, `theta_jc_top`, `theta_sa`, `interface`, `contact_area` and the top sheet (`checks.py`); tests fix that `tj_max`, `psi_jb` and `power_tc` work on a source (`tests/test_source_fields.py`).
* **Interface cleanups** (`tests/test_ui_cleanup.py`): `results.MODEL_LABELS` and `model_label` moved from the GUI to the core so that the comparison plot uses the names; `plots.engineering_ticks` puts `LogLocator(base=1000)` on a log axis of more than six decades; `ResultsView` tab bar `ElideNone` and scroll buttons; Preferences 980 x 600; the ruler of `canvas.py` leaves out a label that would reach the corner.

* **Unknowns of the layered models counted as built**: `fvm.unknowns` and `fdm.unknowns` (laminate nodes of a one-layer board, block layers, the FDM nodes at the corners of the cells) feed the number shown under the cell size (`tests/test_unknown_count.py`: equal to `Network.nl x nx x ny` for six boards at three cells).
* **Via diameter labelled as the drill** (`gui/tips.py` key `drill diameter`, the *Drill diameter* field, `Via.diameter`, the guide, `docs/models.md`): the text now says what the model uses; no number changes.

* **Top path only on a component**: `Heater.has_top_path()` (a component with `theta > 0` and `theta_sa > 0`) is the one condition used by `top_resistance()` and so by the report row and the split of the power; a pure source that carries `theta` and `theta_sa` from a file or the API ignores them (it used to show `r_top > 0` with no heat through the top, and with an interface under it a hidden top path that cooled the part by 39 K in the test case) (`tests/test_top_path_role.py`).

* **Metal block beyond the board outline**: the block is cut at the outline in the FVM, FDM and 3D solid (`blocks.extra_layers` receives the board cover; `solid3d.py`), so a block over the cut-away part no longer drains into the cells pinned to the ambient (it read 38 K cold on the test board: FVM 66.8 against 104.7 degC); the input check measures the block against the outline and says it is cut there. Lumped and Analytic take the block whole on the rectangle of the same area (`tests/test_block_outline.py`).
