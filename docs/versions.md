# Version history

What was implemented in every version (with the module where it lives and how it is tested) and what we still want to implement (last chapter, "Planned"). The release notes are `CHANGELOG.md`; the physics is in `models.md` and `theory.md`, the numbers in `validation.md`. **Newest first.** `docs/version-comparison.md` runs the same 17 boards on every version from v2.0.0 to the tree: what each version implemented, why the numbers moved and the error of each model against an independent 3D solver (regenerated, reviewer pending). Dates are those of the release tags. Each bullet reads: feature (code; tests).

---

## Summary of the current version (2.6.0, 2026-11-07)

* **Models**: exact cover of the board outline and cut-cell links with an edge film (FVM, 3D solid); a laminate band along the board edge; 3D solid slabs by thickness; the Analytic per-layer edge and local transient; a capped first ring and a near-edge warning in the Lumped model; a longer via barrel; the face turned down on a 1/5 law; fast square waves. Several move numbers on purpose (`tests/regression/data/v2.6.0.json`).
* **Program**: the project package `.pcbtz`, a single Layer box, the Display view modes on the board, shorter button hints.
* **Next**: 2.7.0 (fixes and a GUI redesign), see 'Planned: 2.7.0' below; every version is listed newest first after this summary.

* **Fast power waves: the closed-form swing as a floor and the ripple of a fast heater** (`fix/fast-wave-ripple`, from the closed `research/fast-wave-oscillation` (reviewer: GO); created from `feature/integration-260`, where the code is, an exception to the from-`develop` rule; reviewed). The node structure puts the capacity of about 0.77 mm of laminate on each copper node, so the layered models understate the swing of a wave whose shortest stretch is below a few seconds 2 to 3 times at 1 to 3 Hz and 8 to 12 times from 50 Hz to 1 kHz, in the decision and in a resolved curve alike (0.741 K against 6.375 K for 20 Hz, 30 %, 10 W on a 10 mm part). **(1)** `pcbthermal/stackimpedance.py` (theory 10.5): the closed-form impedance of the stack under the part (tanh layer recursion read from the real stack-up, the heater's copper a slab of the column, two-sided for every layer, Bessel disc term, Lanczos-smoothed harmonic sum, 2 ms; `profile_coefficients` for tables) for a part with copper under it (fill at least 0.9, no metal block). **(2)** `profiles.decide` takes `max(node swing, closed form)` for a repeating square wave or table, at every period and for every model of the step response (`HeaterDecision.ripple_swing`). **(3)** A heater over the budget whose shortest stretch is below 4 s (2 s with three or more layers; `profiles.FAST_STRETCH_S`) gets the regime `average + ripple`: not time-stepped, the board run holds its average power, and the zero-mean wave is added at the heater (`profiles.run_windows`, `_add_ripple`, `_ripple_only`: curve averaged over each step, peak and minimum from the envelope, band in the profile plot, `ProfileResult.ripple`, ripple only at the heater and never in the frames or the peak map). With only ripple heaters the result is one window of 600 samples and one frame. **(4)** `api.response_cell_mm`: the decision's step response of a part with a profile is computed at a quarter of its narrow side when that is below the old cell (0.25 mm to the old cell, memory guard respected); a 2 mm part read 0.67 of the truth at 0.1 Hz. **(5)** `checks.check_fast_wave` (live): a wave of 1 Hz or more on a part with no copper under it or copper under only part of it (0.73 at 1 Hz, 2.2 times too small between 3 and 50 Hz, 2.4 times too large at 1 kHz; the review lowered the threshold from 3 Hz) a wave slower than 1 Hz on a part closer to a board side than its radius (0.61 at 0.1 Hz), a fast wave on a board with a metal block and a one-shot table with a short stretch. Tests: `tests/test_stackimpedance.py` (closed forms, independent radial solution, the research numbers, statuses, decision, run, warnings); `tests/test_profiles.py` and `tests/test_gui_profile.py` moved (U1 100 Hz 0.393 K to 2.013 K, `average + ripple`; the mixed-regimes test now compares the ripple with the time-stepped node structure). Evidence: `docs/validation.md` 37. Frozen numbers: none moves. **Review** (independent Cartesian 3D reference of `research/fast-wave-oscillation`, `validation.md` 37): the heater's copper had been lumped as a capacity (0.90 of the reference at 3 kHz, duty 10 %; now a slab, 0.995 to 1.08 on 636 full-copper cases); power tables had no floor and no ripple (a 20 Hz three-level table averaged at 0.66 K against 5.09 K; now 0.998 to 1.015 on four tables); a ripple-only run crashed with the Lumped and Analytic models (fixed, tests added); warnings for a metal block and a one-shot table added. Three mappings (`ImportedCopper.mapping`): **faces** (default; `copper.face_conductances`, transmissibility upscaling: one local conduction problem on the pixels between two cell centres per pair of neighbours, FVM and FDM), **lambda** (the geometric mean of the Wiener bounds per cell, much faster, better on 45 degree tracks at about ten pixels per cell) and **fill** (the arithmetic fill, the old path, kept for comparison). **The faces are built on the REDUCED sheet**: the network is built from the cell fill first, so the laminate rule plans its interior laminate nodes on the real copper of each cell and moves a sheet conductance dS out of the copper node of the flagged cells; dS is then taken off every pixel of the cell, copper or bare, before the faces are computed (faces from the unreduced sheet count that laminate twice and are worse than the rule off; a test holds the order). The FVM and FDM take the faces (the FDM windows are centred on the nodes and the edge rows get half faces); the cell fill of the imported layers comes from the masks (box average, `resample_coverage`) for the vertical links, the heat capacity and every other model. The **3D solid** reads the fill (average conductivity) and warns; the **Lumped and Analytic** read only the fill and ALWAYS warn (too cool, a sample and not a bound: 1 to 16 % in the study of 24 mm windows, 6 % on average, 17 to 42 % on the whole RP2040 board, `docs/validation.md` 27.7); the FVM and FDM carry one fixed note on the accuracy (and a warning above a 1 mm cell, or below 8 pixels per cell). A cell is almost never a whole number of pixels: the face problems sample the raster at the centres of a grid at least as fine as the raster (`sample_coverage`) instead of averaging it, which keeps the clearances between copper areas (averaging bridged them and read 1.4 to 3.9 % of the rise cool on twelve real windows at cells of 0.92 and 1.09 mm; found by the review, the unaligned-cells defect); `pixels_per_cell` rounds up to an even number, at most `MAX_PIXELS_PER_CELL` = 24 (a finer raster is averaged down). **The import** (`copper.import_copper`, no GUI): rasterises the copper layers one at a time (only the packed text is kept), clears the copper outside a non-rectangular outline, turns plated round holes into vias (slots and unplated holes are said in the warnings) and refuses a panel; the default pixel is `min(m w / 4, cell / 10)` for `m` sub-pixels per pixel and `w` the thinnest feature (a quarter of the thinnest feature, and at most a tenth of the cell, so that the faces have about ten pixels per cell). **Memory** (`memory.py`): `copper_run_bytes` (the unpacked masks held, 1 byte per pixel and layer, and the working set of the face construction, 40 bytes per source pixel of the largest layer plus 50 MB; added to the base of the run), `import_draw_bytes` and `check_import` (refuses BEFORE anything is drawn and names the pixel that fits). **Hostile input** (a project file is hostile): the shape of a mask is checked first (at most 400 million pixels), the text is decoded strictly, the deflated stream is inflated with a cap of the bytes the shape needs plus one (a zip bomb is refused), the extent of the masks must match the board box (a board resized after the import is refused with a message instead of putting the copper in the wrong place), duplicate and out-of-range layer indices are refused. **Saving**: a problem that holds imported copper but no import record (made by `ImportedBoard.to_problem`) used to lose the masks silently on save and reopen as a bare board; it is now written in a record of its own (`Project.run_problem`, `project_to_dict`). `checks.check_input` reads the imported fill and warns on a zone drawn on an imported layer. No interface yet (the import dialog is the next branch, see 'Planned'). **Known limit**: on a 24 mm window the FVM overshoots by +7.9 to +9.6 % at a 2 mm cell (see `validation.md` 27.3 for the cause); the FVM memory law ignores the vias (a separate fix, 'Planned').

Evidence: `research/component-heatsink/` (README, NOTES.md; the notes that were in `research.md` are kept there).
