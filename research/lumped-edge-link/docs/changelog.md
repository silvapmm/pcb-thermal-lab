# Changelog text (when adopted)

* Fixed: the Lumped model read a part near a side or a corner of the board too cool (on average -9 % of the rise over a sweep of 432 cases, up to -22 %); the link from the footprint to the first ring now uses the potential of the part and its mirror images in the sides of the board, and the zoned footprint the same. The average error of the sweep is -2.5 % (worst cool -8.6 %); a part next to a cooled side in a corner can read up to 9 % too hot. Lumped numbers of every board move (hottest point -0.5 to +3.1 % of the rise on the 17 reference boards, mostly hotter).
