### 55.7 Surface against the layer mean: when the choice matters (`research/readout-conventions`)

The closed series `research/readout-conventions/lib/plate_series.py` (plate W x W x t, centred square source, adiabatic top, film below) matches the independent 3D solver within 1 % (surface) and 5 % (surface minus mean) on a 5 mm plate (k 150, h 400) under a 10 mm source, and at the geometry of six 50 mm plates gives 10.76 / 11.82 / 0.03 / 41.1 / 13.31 / 52.4 % of the surface rise against the 3D 10.72 / 11.83 / 0.04 / 41.39 / 13.44 / 53.68 % (2 mm Cu at 1000 W/m2K, 1.5 mm Al at 2000, 3 mm Cu in air, 10 mm Al at 5000, the plate of case 2 with one centred source, 10 mm of k 50). The program's FVM node equals the 3D column mean within 0.04 K on the six plates and the base node of a copper-core board within 0.001 K at 0.25 mm. Thin copper: the harness bound `q'' t / 2k` is 0.57 to 3.79 mK (0.0009 to 0.005 % of the rise, 20 boards); the independent 3D of a simplified stack with the heater flux of three of them (example_board, plate_pad, part_top_path: 1.5 W on 6 mm, 5 W on 8 mm, 3 W on 6 mm; full 35 um copper / 1.53 mm FR-4 / 35 um, 50 mm board, h 10) gives 1.37 / 2.62 / 2.74 mK, 0.72 to 0.74 of the bound. The share depends on Bi = h t / k, on t/a and on the plate width: on a plate 5 a wide 3.5 % (2 mm copper core) to 41 % (10 mm Al cold plate); the same cooled plates 50 mm wide 10.7 to 54 %. Numbers: `research/readout-conventions/README.md` 4 and 7.
