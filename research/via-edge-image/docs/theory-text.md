# Text for `docs/theory.md` 2.6 (limits) and `docs/validation.md`: the via near a board edge

(To be adopted only if the mirror image is implemented. Figure: `research/via-edge-image/figures/fig_01_via_near_edge.png`, to be copied to `docs/images/`. Source: Yovanovich (1995), R, equations 9 and 10, for the image of a point source at a plane; the lattice form is derived here and checked against a resolved reference.)

**The via near an edge.** The lattice carries the sheet outside the footprint, edge included, but the footprint sits on cell centres and the via does not: for a 0.3 mm via 0.16 mm from an insulated edge on 1 mm cells the true image of the via is at `D_true = 2 max(delta, r_v) = 0.32 mm` and the image of the footprint has `D_lat = 1.0 mm`. The mean potential of the footprint therefore misses `(I / 2 pi S) ln(D_lat / D_true)`, and the well carries it: `G_c = 2 pi S / (ln(r_eff / r_t) + c)`, `c = sum over the nearest wall of each axis of [ln D_lat - ln D_true]` (plus the diagonal image at a corner), with `ln D_lat = ln dx + (pi/2)(P_img - kappa)` and `P_img = sum_ab w_a w_b a(x_a - x'_b)` between the footprint cells and the mirror images of the cell centres. A negative `c` (a small via whose footprint was enlarged to `r_eff = r_t`) enlarges the footprint until `ln(r_eff/r_t) + c > 0`. Walls are the box edges or axis-parallel segments of the outline within six cells; the FDM (half-volume edge nodes) and the 3D solid have no image.

**Measured** (10 x 10 mm board, four copper planes, layered reference 1/16 mm; via effect, 1 / 0.5 / 0.25 mm cells): nearest via 0.16 mm from the edge +14.5 / +6.2 / +0.6 % without the image, +0.7 / +0.1 / -0.2 % with it; corner +26.0 / +9.4 % without, -1.0 / -0.7 % with; outline on a grid line +14.1 / +5.9 % and +1.1 / +0.1 %, inside a cell +16.5 / +7.3 % and +2.5 / +0.3 %. For an edge film the image holds to `h_side` about 1000 W/m2K; beyond it the via effect near the edge is below 1 % of the rise and the error is that of the cooled edge, not of the via. The error without the image is 1 % of the part's rise at 1 mm, on the cool side.
