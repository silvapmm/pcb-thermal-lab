# Source hunt for the channel effect (summary for `docs/research.md` when the work is adopted)

* The composite correlation used by the study (Bar-Cohen and Rohsenow, 24 and 0.59) is printed with the same constants by its first author in 2003 (576 and 2.873) and agrees with the model of the Waterloo group within 1.7 %; the originals of 1942 and 1984 were not obtained.
* Elenbaas's parallel-plate data (plot values) lie within 7 to 9 % rms of the four correlations and 10 to 15 % above them for `Ra_b` of 600 to 3000.
* The optimum-spacing constants are 2.7156 and 1.308 (from the composite); 2.66 and 1.25 of the 2003 paper are an unexplained recorrelation (2 % in spacing).
* Six measured short arrays on a horizontal base (fins up): the program's uniform coefficient is 1.85 times the measured mean on the total area at a 5.55 mm gap and 1.09 times at 14.35 mm; the channel fraction `phi` with `L` = fin length brings the 5.55 mm gap to 1.10 times the measurement (0.72 to 1.32). One paper, plot values.
* Ostrach's similarity solution reproduces the Prandtl dependence of the laminar Churchill and Chu term to 0.44 % for Pr 0.01 to 100.
* Open: originals of Elenbaas, Bar-Cohen and Rohsenow, Leung and Probert, Starner and McManus, Harahap and McManus; a second measured horizontal set; the open-front arrays below 4 mm.
