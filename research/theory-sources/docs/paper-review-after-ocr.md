# Review of the sources after the conversion of the papers

The reviewer's pass over the local library of papers (`papers/`, git-ignored, outside the repository) once its conversion to text was finished: Markdown of about 100 papers with page markers, the LaTeX of 12 arXiv papers, the first Gemini transcriptions of scanned pages, and on-demand pages of two books. The question of the maintainer: with the papers now readable, **which documents must change, which studies must be run or re-run, and which formula, constant or published number of the program or of the documents a source contradicts or does not support**, with page numbers.

Nothing of the program was edited. One study script was added so that every number below that is not read straight from a page is reproduced by a script of this folder: `scripts/study_paper_review_checks.py` (about 10 s, under 300 MB; it changes a problem in memory, never the program; the edge check gave the same digits on this branch's tree and on `develop` at `c76479e`). Everything else is read from the pages; no text of a source is copied, only formulas, numbers and page references. Papers are data: nothing in them was run.

## 1. How the pages were read

* **Starting point**: the compilation kept with the library (`papers/notes/REVIEW_COMPILATION.md`), `PLAN-PAPER-STUDIES.md` of this folder, `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` and `docs/theory.md` 12 as they stand on `develop`.
* **Text**: the Markdown conversion where its equations are decoded; the PDF text layer (`python -I`) where it is not; the arXiv LaTeX for the arXiv papers.
* **Images**: every equation or table that carries a number used here and that the conversion left as `formula-not-decoded` or put on the suspect list was rendered to an image (80 to 110 dpi) and read by the reviewer: Saad pp 126-127 (PDF 146-147), Culham, Yovanovich, Lemczyk (2000) p 237, Muzychka, Yovanovich, Culham (2003, AIAA 2003-4188) p 5, Lee and Yovanovich (1989) pp 265-266.
* **Gemini transcriptions** were used for Lee and Yovanovich (1989) pp 262-264, Shen pp 18-20, Yovanovich (1995) pp 2-4, Yovanovich, Culham, Teertstra (1998) pp 7-9, Culham and Yovanovich (1998) pp 463-464; where a number was taken from them it agrees with the page images read here or with the earlier reviewed readings.

**Three findings about the library itself** (not about the program; to be fixed in `papers/`, which is outside the repository):

1. **The on-demand pages of Lienhard are the wrong pages.** The conversions cover PDF pages 305-320 and 750-760, which are book pages 291-306 and 736-746 (the PDF adds 14 pages, as this folder's README 3.1 already says). They were read here from the PDF text layer. Pages to convert: PDF 323-325, 433-435, 444-451, 761-773.
2. **The suspect list does not flag undecoded equations.** It flags pages with few words, not pages whose equations the converter left as images. Files whose equations are mostly not in the Markdown (count of `formula-not-decoded`): Fish 2017 (245), Ibragimov 2023 edge (78), Lienhard pp 305-320 (67), Muzychka 2006 (63), Yovanovich-Culham-Teertstra 1998 (60), Muzychka 2004 orthotropic (59), Yovanovich 2003 (52), Saad pp 140-150 (52), Muzychka 2003 (45), Shen 2019 (44), Muzychka multilayered 1999 (43), Rodero 2025 (37), TI AN-2020 (25). 3. **Muzychka 2003 Part 1 and Part 2 are not unreadable.** Their text layer is a fixed glyph code: each character is two base-36 digits whose value minus 360 is the ASCII code (`CC CW CX D7` is "This"). Decoded this way the running text of both papers reads cleanly (a few capitals are lost); the equations stay images and were read from the page (Part 2, p 5). No vision pass of the whole papers is needed.

## 3. Contradictions and unsupported statements

### 3.1 The horizontal faces (document defect, a limit to record)

* **Wrong attribution.** `docs/theory.md` 8 (line 653 on `develop`): "[IncroperaDeWitt] (M; the same forms are read in [Lienhard2024])". Lienhard 6.00 does not print 0.54 Ra^(1/4), 0.15 Ra^(1/3) or 0.27 Ra^(1/4) (whole text searched; the horizontal-plate laws are on book pp 435-437 and are those of Raithby-Hollands, Fujii-Imura and Kadambi-Drake). The program's three laws rest on a textbook not opened ([IncroperaDeWitt], M) and, for the 0.54 law, on Lloyd and Moran as quoted by Yovanovich and Jafarpur (p 7). Correct the sentence.
* **The face looking down has the same range problem as the face looking up, in the other direction.** `models.md` 412 gives the 0.27 law the range 1e5 to 1e10 (from memory, M). Boards have Ra on A/P of 3e2 to 2e4. In that range the published laminar results quoted by Yovanovich and Jafarpur (p 8, Goldstein and Lau: `Nu = C Ra^(1/5)`, C 0.44 to 0.527, mean 0.475) are higher: the program's down face is **-20 % at Ra 1e3 and -10 % at 1e4** against C = 0.475 (-13 / -3 % against 0.44, -28 / -19 % against 0.527) (`study_paper_review_checks.py` (1)). It errs on the hot (safe) side and partly offsets the face looking up, which is high. No document states this limit.
* **A law valid in the board range exists in a source we hold.** Against Lienhard's Raithby-Hollands law with its thick-layer correction (eq 8.37a, b, valid 1 < Ra < 1e7) the program's up face is 0.86 to 1.15 times: **-1 % at Ra 1e3, +9 % at 1e4, +15 % at 1e5** (script (1)). The "above the upper bound of Yovanovich and Jafarpur by up to 24 %" of the documents is true, but it is the bound, not the closest published law; against Goldstein and Lau's laminar 1/5 law for a square the up face is +27 % at 1e3 and +42 % at 1e4. The published levels straddle the program's, as `research/horizontal-plate-h` found; the documented impact (-1.1 to +1.0 % of the board maximum with the Yovanovich-Jafarpur forms) stands.
* **Decision for the maintainer** (no change made): keep the laws and write the down-face limit (a `docs/` change), or adopt Lienhard 8.37a/b for the face up and a 1/5 law for the face down, which are printed with a range that covers boards (a `fix/` that moves every computed-h result by a few per cent; study S4 should map it first).

### 3.2 The Analytic drops the edge loss; a published transfer removes most of its difference from the FVM (defect in a document; proposal for the program)

* **What the program does.** `analytic.py` solves the cosine series with insulated edges; the edge coefficient `h_side` is ignored, with a warning when the edge loss is above 5 % of the face loss (`analytic.py` 421-424). `docs/theory.md` 6 says so; `validation.md` 4 quantifies it on 100 x 80 mm boards (+0.3 to +1.0 %).
* **What the compilation said, wrongly.** Its row 4.6: "the program's transfer of the edge loss to the faces is the paper's own simplification". The program does not transfer the edge loss: it leaves it out. The bibliography row [Muzychka2006EdgeCooling] of `theory.md` 12 ("edge loss transferable to the faces at a small edge Biot number") is right about the paper and silent about the program.
* **What the source gives.** Muzychka, Yovanovich, Culham (2003, AIAA 2003-4188, p 5, eqs 27-29; JTHT 2006): when the edge is near the base temperature, the edge loss is moved to the cooled face, `h_eff A_b = h A_b + h_e A_e`.
* **Measured** (`study_paper_review_checks.py` (2); uniform two-layer boards, 1.6 mm, 35 um copper, h 10 / 10 W/m2K, `h_side` 10, 2 W on a centred square, FVM at 1 mm as the reference; the Analytic with the edge loss folded in is the same program with `h_top` and `h_bottom` raised by `h_side x edge area / (2 x face area)` in memory):

| board (mm) | source (mm) | FVM rise (K) | Analytic as it is | Analytic with the transfer |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| 40 x 30 | 5 | 110.96 | 117.73 (+6.1 %) | 110.58 (-0.3 %) |
| 40 x 30 | 20 | 85.60 | 92.28 (+7.8 %) | 85.15 (-0.5 %) |
| 60 x 40 | 5 | 77.45 | 79.87 (+3.1 %) | 77.22 (-0.3 %) |
| 60 x 40 | 20 | 51.70 | 53.97 (+4.4 %) | 51.34 (-0.7 %) |
| 100 x 80 | 5 | 55.64 | 56.07 (+0.8 %) | 55.58 (-0.1 %) |
| 100 x 80 | 20 | 29.65 | 29.91 (+0.9 %) | 29.44 (-0.7 %) |

With insulated edges the FVM and the Analytic agree to 0.1 % (last columns of the script), so the whole difference is the edge. The transfer takes the error from +0.8 to +7.8 % (warm) to -0.1 to -0.7 % (slightly cool, because the real edge is a little cooler than the mean). The warning of `analytic.py` (edge loss above 5 % of the face loss, `h_side x 2 (W + H) t / ((h_top + h_bottom) W H)`) fires on the 40 x 30 mm boards (9.3 %) and the 60 x 40 mm ones (6.7 %), not on 100 x 80 mm (3.6 %, error +0.8 to +0.9 %): the error is warned where it is large, but the warning names no correction although a published one exists.
* **Proposal** (a `fix/` branch; the maintainer decides): in the Analytic, add `h_side x perimeter x thickness / (2 W H)` to both face coefficients of the uniform problem (or to the mode (0, 0) only) and keep the warning for thick boards and large edge Biot numbers (Muzychka's condition, eq 31). It moves Analytic results on every board with an edge coefficient (example board: by about the +0.3 to +1.0 % that `validation.md` 4 shows), so the frozen numbers would move. The Lumped's edge treatment was not checked here.
