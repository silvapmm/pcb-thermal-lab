# Plan of the paper studies: new studies from the sources now held, their link with the earlier studies, and the bibliography of the project documents

The reviewer's plan, written for the maintainer and the agents who will carry it out after the current 2.5.0 work. It rests on `README.md` sections 8 and 9 of this folder, the compilation kept with the local copies of the papers (outside the repository: sections 3 to 6) and `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`. Nothing here was run except two checks of seconds (marked). Nothing in `pcbthermal/`, `tests/` or `docs/` is changed by this plan.

## 0. Before any new study: two defects of the reading pass

* **Numbers that no script in the repository reproduces (defect in a study).** Every comparison of `README.md` section 9 (the face bounds of Yovanovich and Jafarpur and their impact on four boards, the forced law against Yovanovich and Teertstra, the air properties against Lienhard Table A.6, the TI via, the strip Table 2 of Yovanovich, Muzychka and Culham, the Krogh and lattice-radius checks to 1e-12) was made by scratch scripts kept outside the repository (`conv_check.py`, `conv_impact.py`). Section 9 says so, but under the folder's rule ("everything must be reproducible by running a script in the folder") they are not yet results. **Study S0 below turns them into study scripts and tests before anything is built on them.**
* **One of them was wrong (corrected now).** The TI AN-2020 via was quoted as 259.4 C/W with the program's rule (-0.6 %). `Via(diameter = 0.3048 mm).thermal_area(17.5 um, 400 W/mK)` with the 1.65 mm length gives **261.16 C/W (+0.06 %)**, open or air-filled core alike; plating outside the drill gives 232.8. The agreement is better than stated; `README.md` 9.4 and the bibliography entry [TI-AN2020] are corrected. The compilation outside the repository (its row 4.8 and the summary "-0.6 %") must get the same correction.
