# Research summary, phase 1: Gerber import, reading the files

Ready to paste into the project `docs/research.md` as a section when the maintainer adopts the work. Everything is reproducible from `research/gerber-import/` (README sections, `CONCLUSIONS.md`); nothing in `pcbthermal/` was changed.

## Open now: Gerber import, reading the files

**Answers** (numbers in the README of the folder):

* **Reading: done without libraries.** `lib/native_gerber.py` (NumPy only) draws copper layers and reads drills: same raster as gerbonara + shapely at the pixel, 161 of 164 comparable files of gerbonara's test set agree within 3 % of the area, 34 of 34 drill files read by both identical, X2 attributes (file function, aperture functions, net names) read (README 4.17 to 4.19, 4.22, 4.24).
* **Vias and nets** (4.29): holes from the drill file matched to pads by position, aperture function and net; 225 of 225 vias of a real 4-layer board have one net on the four layers.
* **Nets against a CAD file** (4.33): Stormduino Gerbers + `.kicad_pcb`: 1380/1380 track points, 48/48 via points and 281/281 pad points on our copper; no piece or joined group with two nets; 55 of 56 nets without a zone fill split like the CAD file.
* **Nets against the CAD file, pixel by pixel** (4.46, figures 13 and 14): the `.kicad_pcb` drawn on the grid by net against the Gerber copper: 99.998 % of the 2.25 million CAD pixels lie on Gerber copper, 0.13 % and 0.16 % of the Gerber copper has no CAD object farther than a pixel.
* **Import** (`lib/importer.py`): box, binary and coverage mask per layer, holes with pad, kind and net, warnings; no program needed.
* **The same copper, pixel by pixel** (4.45, figures 9 to 12): four tools draw the Stormduino top layer; against the vector truth the independent reader differs in 72 of 4.49 million pixels (pcb-tools 9,084, pygerber 68,445 after my own alignment and threshold), and on 12 layers of 8 boards in 122 of 4.76 million, all within 0.045 pixel of an exact edge. The comparison found a defect of our vector reference (arcs ignored), now fixed.
* **Bench boards** (4.30): our IC board with circular tracks, eight nets, thermal vias and a drill file, and the rectangle board; generated by the repository (no licence issue).

**Not validated**: the reader against a manufactured board; the nets on more than one board and CAD format; the Ucamco specification was not read in full.

**Open decisions for the maintainer**: the layer/outline confirmation step; board outline polygons; whether to add other CAD formats for the net check.

Pictures (`../figures/`): `fig_01_gerber_render.png`, and the comparison of the tools in `fig_09` to `fig_12`.

* **Validation report** (`REPORT.md`, README 4.41): the converter on all 12 copper layers of the 8 boards: area 0.14 % (0.05 % with a coverage mask), connected pieces identical on 12 of 12 layers, 109 of 109 holes on the Stormduino, nets agree with the KiCad file; differences from gerbonara, pcb-tools and pygerber tabulated.
