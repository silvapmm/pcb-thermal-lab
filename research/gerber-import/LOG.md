# Log, phase 1: reading the files

The study started as one and was split in three; the first entries are the log of the single study, shared by the three folders; the rest belong to this phase.

- Branch `research/gerbers` created at the maintainer's request. Gerber import studied: gerbonara and shapely installed and run on the Stormduino board (Arduino Uno clone, MIT); the Gerber converts exactly to polygons; the raster is checked against the exact area; the mappings of copper to cell conductances (fill fraction, power means, Wiener bounds, lambda, Hashin-Shtrikman, Bruggeman, cell problem, flow-based faces) compared on 42 windows of 7 downloaded boards; the cautions (thick copper, heater size, time response, hatched zones, two and four layers) studied; the independent NumPy reader written and checked against gerbonara. The code of FYPA, padne and altium_monkey (AGPL) was read for ideas only.
- Lumped and Analytic models tested with the mapping; reader conformance on gerbonara's 272 test files (149 of 164 comparable agree); four reader defects found and fixed; drill files compared position by position (inverted `TZ`/`LZ` found: the first comparison had matched only the counts); nets (`TO.N`) and the Joule heat of a net; tracks at an angle; comparison with gerbonara, pygerber and pcb-tools; Ucamco's `gerber-writer` as a third writer; the regime of Sampson's article; the literature notes; the conclusions compiled.
- Coverage masks, every model of the program (a first table had used the unmodified program by mistake and was redone with an additive-union copy), the program's union rule by maximum found and tested, vias and nets matched from the files, an analogue of Sampson's post-mounted board.
- Pictures: the first versions of figures 02 and 03 rendered boards of gerbonara's test set whose licences could not be confirmed and were replaced (a synthetic board of our own, a window of the Stormduino board); `lib/our_board.py` added (an IC board of our own with circular tracks, nets, vias and a drill file) with figures 04 to 06.
- Folder arranged in the layout of the other research folders: documents in the root, `scripts/` (all executable Python, run from the repository root), `lib/` (reader, mappings, scenarios, nets, our board), `tests/`, `figures/`, `docs/`; this log added.
- Aligned with `develop` for the merge: `develop` merged into the branch (no conflicts) and a trial merge of the branch into `develop` is clean; `docs/` rewritten as clean texts to apply (roadmap, changelog, decisions, research summary); the 42 Python files of `scripts/` and `lib/` pass the documentation gate of `tests/test_docs.py`.
- **Split into three folders** at the maintainer's request: phase 1 `research/gerber-import` (reading), phase 2 `research/gerber-models` (the copper in the models), phase 3 `research/gerber-nets` (nets with voltage and current).
