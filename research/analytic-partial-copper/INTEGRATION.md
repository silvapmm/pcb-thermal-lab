# Entering the program: the Analytic model on partial copper

**Status (audit 2.7.0).** P1 is implemented in 2.4.5 (`fix/model-limit-warnings`: the copper-spread trigger of `analytic._effective_sheet` is the larger of the spread at `lambda_l` and the spread with the full-copper fin length, as section 9.4 of the README recommends). P2 is not implemented; P3 (the limit stated) is in `docs/validation.md` and `docs/theory.md` 6. The item stays open as A2 of `research/ROADMAP.md`. 'The work is not in the program' below is the status when written.

The work is not in the program. Nothing here edits `pcbthermal/`; the candidate is `lib/defect.py`, measured on copies. Three proposals, in the order of cost; the evidence is in README sections 4 to 6.

## P1. A better warning: a new trigger and a measured text (no physics; corrected by the review)

* **What the review found.** The Analytic's input warning (spread of the K0-weighted fill above 0.25, `analytic._effective_sheet`) fires on 16 of the 18 pad boards of README 4.5, but on the 52 pad rows of `gap-subdivision` it is **silent on the two worst rows** (`gap0.1-empty` -66.7 %, `gap0.2-empty` -62.0 %) and on six more rows of -4.0 to -6.9 % (README 9.4). On a thin gap the fin length `lambda_l = sqrt(S_mean / (h + g_adjacent))` is about a millimetre, the weights stay under the part, the pad edge is not seen, and the sheet is taken as copper. A text-only P1 would leave the worst case silent.
* **The trigger.** For every copper layer that carries a heater, compute the spread twice with the same K0 weights: with the program's `lambda_l`, and with the length of the same layer as full copper, `lambda_cu,l = sqrt(S_cu,l / (h_surface,l + g_adjacent,l))`, `S_cu,l = k_cu t_cu,l + k_lam (slab_l - t_cu,l)`; warn when the larger of the two exceeds 0.25. Measured on 119 boards (the 20 boards of README 3, the 52 pad and 44 uniform rows of `gap-subdivision`, the three example boards; `scripts/study_review.py` part d): no board with an error beyond 5 % stays silent (the program's trigger leaves 5); the uniform rows and the example boards stay silent (largest second spread 0.14 on the example board, whose Analytic is -1.9 % of the FVM); 5 warned boards are within 2 %. Any threshold from 0.15 to 0.30 gives the same result on these boards (below 0.14 the example board would be warned): keep 0.25, the program's value.
* **The text (proposed, exact):** `copper coverage varies around the heaters (spread {s:.2f}): the Analytic model spreads the copper of each layer into one uniform sheet and has no copper edge; on a part over a copper pad or a window it was measured between 76 % too cool and 9 % too hot against a resolved 3D solution, and the same board entered as one layer or as two (bare second layer) differed by up to 1.8 times. Use the FVM result for this board.` (76 %: two layers, bare second, 10 mm pad, laminate 0.3 W/mK in the plane, README 9.1; 9 %: one layer, 40 mm pad; 1.8: README 4.4.)
* **Impact.** Results bit-identical; more boards warned (70 of 119 against 60). Tests: the trigger fires on the `gap0.1-empty` board of `gap-subdivision` and stays silent on the example board and on a uniform board; `tests/test_help.py`, the user guide on the Analytic model.
* **Risk.** None to the results. It does not remove the error.

## P2. The candidate in the Analytic model, steady state (a `feature/` item)

* **What.** For a layer whose copper is not uniform: (a) laminate nodes through the thickness as extra layers of the modal system (a one-layer board gets `n` laminate nodes instead of the series film `laminate.series_bottom_problem`; a multilayer board gets `n - 1` interior nodes per gap), the same node construction as the FVM (`laminate.py`; A1 of `gap-subdivision` and A11 of the roadmap decide `n`: the Analytic must use the number the FVM uses, because the candidate equals the FVM with the same nodes to 0.1 to 0.7 % when the copper edges lie on tile edges, review); (b) the series solved for the weakest sheet of each node (the bare laminate), the copper put back as a lateral conductance on the links of a tile grid through the tile Green-function system that `solve_steady` already has for vias, blocks and heatsinks (`C` gets lateral entries); (c) the K0-weighted fill and its warning no longer decide the sheet of a layer that has the defect; a layer with uniform copper takes the program's path unchanged.
* **Measured impact** (README 5, `data/`):
  * 52 pad rows of the baseline of `gap-subdivision` (graded 3D reference): Analytic -66.7 to +1.3 % (mean absolute 17.9 %, 32 rows beyond 10 %), candidate with 2 nodes -5.7 to +0.5 % (1.68 %, none beyond 10 %), with 4 nodes -2.9 to +2.1 % (0.83 %, 46 rows where it runs).
  * 18 pad boards of 6 to 40 mm (60 mm board, one and two layers, three second layers): program -65.3 to +8.6 %; candidate with 4 nodes -2.5 to +1.4 % at a 1 mm tile (-5.5 to -1.5 % at a converged tile for pads of 6 to 20 mm, README 5.4).
  * 13 out-of-sample cases (board, part, thickness, films, copper, laminate): -0.4 to -3.6 % against -4.6 to -46.1 %.
  * Off-centre pad, part off the pad, two pads: -1.5 to -2.6 % with 4 nodes against -21 to -22 %; a 6 mm window in full copper: -6.0 % against -56.7 % (the FVM -20.2 %).
  * 44 uniform rows: unchanged by n = 1 (the program's result), 0.29 to 0.06 % mean absolute at n = 2; a uniform board with the simple stack-up moves by 0.3 to 0.6 points (the candidate uses the advanced stack-up's slabs).
* **What does not change.** The Lumped, FVM, FDM and the 3D solid; the Analytic on boards with copper everywhere (to 0.3 points), the transient (see risks), the API.
* **Risks and open points.**
  * **Cost**: the dense tile system has (tiles with a defect) x (nodes carrying one) unknowns: one layer, 1 mm tiles, pads of 10 / 20 / 40 mm: 140 / 480 / 1760; with a 0.5 mm tile (needed for a part of 3 mm) 480 / 1760 / 6720; a part of 6 mm on a board with 40 x 40 mm of patterned copper is a few thousand unknowns; a Gerber board with many small pads and fine tracks is **not** within reach of this construction (thousands of tiles per layer; the research code stops at 6000). A policy is needed (tile adaptive to the copper, or fall back to the program's path and warn).
  * **The tile rule** (at most a sixth of the part; three tiles across overshoots by 13 to 18 %) was found on two boards; the mixed effect of `n` and the tile needs a sweep on more boards before it is a rule of the program. A coarse tile can be right for the wrong reason.
  * **Copper edges inside a tile** (review, README 9.3): the partly covered tile conducts like copper, so a pad whose edge falls in the middle of a 1 mm tile runs -2.6 to -7.7 % against the FVM with the same nodes (pads of 10.5 to 21.5 mm on the 60 mm board; -3.3 % with 0.5 mm tiles on a 40 mm board). Every board of README 3 to 5 and every row of 5.6 had its edges on tile edges. Real copper never does; the tile would have to follow the copper (about a twentieth of the narrowest copper feature for 1 %), which makes the next point worse.
  * **The tile rule is not monotone** (review, README 9.2): with two to four tiles across the part the candidate is -6.9 to +39.4 % against the FVM with the same nodes; with six it is within 1.2 % only when the edges are aligned (-4.4 % when they are not).
  * **What the candidate is** (review): with one node per gap it equals the program's FVM on the 52 rows (mean 0.33 %, -1.3 to +0.5 %); everything it gains over the program's FVM comes from the laminate nodes, which `gap-subdivision` and `bare-spot-under-source` propose for the FVM itself. With a dense solve of thousands of unknowns, the Analytic would lose what makes it worth having (a fast estimate, and the transient, which the candidate does not cover).
  * **Size on real boards** (review, README 9.5): 2 600 unknowns for a 100 x 80 mm two-layer board with a 60 x 40 mm pour and a 6 mm part at 1 mm tiles, 10 000 with a 3 mm part (0.5 mm tiles), 10 160 with a second pour on four layers; the example board 3 740 without its vias and heatsink, which the candidate does not model.
  * **Combination** with vias, metal blocks, heatsinks (the same Woodbury system: the lateral entries and the vertical ones share `C`; not measured), outlines (the Analytic runs on the equivalent rectangle), heaters on inner layers (the candidate takes the first layer only), several heaters.
  * **The transient** of the Analytic uses the fill and the layer capacity `c_l` of the uniform sheet; it would not follow the steady state.
  * **The layered limit remains**: the candidate is the FVM's model and carries its error (-0.9 to -3.4 % with 4 and 8 nodes on pads of 10 to 20 mm; -3.6 % on a 6 mm pad with 8 nodes), which `gap-subdivision` and `bare-spot-under-source` (P1 to P3 of its INTEGRATION) treat.
  * **Memory**: `n_terms^2 x nodes^2` doubles for the modal matrices (300 x 300 x 15 squared is 160 MB; 8 layers with 2 nodes per gap is 15 nodes).
* **Tests the adoption needs**: the candidate against the FVM with the same nodes on every pad row (0.1 to 0.7 % measured with aligned edges; boards with edges inside tiles too), the uniform boards bit-identical, an energy balance, the independent solver of `tests/helpers/layered3d.py` on the one-layer pad of `tests/test_layered_vs_3d.py` (documented limit -28.8 % there becomes the candidate's), the one-layer and the two-layer-bare entries of the same board within 0.2 points.

## P3. If P2 is not adopted: state the limit

* Keep the program's model and document in `docs/validation.md` and `docs/models.md` the Analytic's behaviour on pad boards (README 3: -65 to +9 %; shortfall for pads up to 20 mm and, on a one-layer board, an excess for 30 and 40 mm; the dependence on how the layers are entered; the dependence on the laminate in-plane conductivity: -9.0 % against -28.3 % for the 20 mm pad with 0.9 and 0.3 W/mK), with P1's warning. The FVM (-0.5 to -18 % on the same boards, better with the laminate nodes of A1 and A11) is the model to use for partial copper.

## The reviewer's recommendation

**P1 with the new trigger, and P3. Not P2.** The candidate is right on the designed boards but it is the FVM's layered model solved by a dense Green-function system: its accuracy over the program's FVM comes only from laminate nodes (the FVM's own proposal), it needs copper edges on tile edges (-3 to -8 % otherwise), its tile rule is not monotone, it grows past the research limit on an ordinary 100 x 80 mm board with pours, and it has not been combined with vias, blocks, outlines, several heaters on inner layers or the transient. That is a limit of the Analytic model to live with, stated in the program and in the documents, with the FVM as the model for partial copper. The text for the documents (P3):

* `docs/validation.md` (Analytic model, the paragraph that now says "within 2.3 % with partial copper ... except a copper island around the heater (-4.3 %, no warning)", which compares with the FVM, not with a resolved 3D solution): "Against a resolved 3D solution, a part on a copper pad over bare laminate is where the Analytic model is weakest: -37 to +9 % on a one-layer 60 mm board with pads of 6 to 40 mm, -65 to -0.6 % on two layers, -67 to +1 % on the 52 pad rows of `research/gap-subdivision` (mean absolute 18 %), and -76 % with a laminate of 0.3 W/mK in the plane. The model replaces the copper of each layer by one uniform sheet (the fill weighted by K0(r / lambda) around the heaters), so it has no copper edge, and on a one-layer board the laminate is a film. The same physical board entered as one layer or as two layers with a bare second one differs by up to 1.8 times. The program warns when the copper around a heater is far from uniform (two spreads, `research/analytic-partial-copper/INTEGRATION.md` P1); use the FVM for such boards."
* `docs/models.md` (the choice table and the limits of the Analytic model): "Analytic: boards whose copper is uniform around the heaters (planes, pours larger than the heater's spreading length). For pads and windows use the FVM: the Analytic was measured 76 % too cool to 9 % too hot there."
* `docs/theory.md` (the effective sheet): one sentence that the K0-weighted fill is a heuristic without a published source and that its error on pads is in `docs/validation.md`.

## Decisions left to the maintainer

1. P1 (the new trigger and the text of the warning, a `fix/` branch, results bit-identical): adopt now?
2. P2 or P3: the reviewer advises P3 (above); P2 only if the maintainer wants the Analytic to carry partial copper in spite of the cost and the alignment limit, after A1 and A11 fix `n`.
3. (If P2) The default `n` of the Analytic follows the FVM's (2 for a one-layer board today). At `n = 2` the candidate is -3.9 to -8.8 % on pads of 14 to 6 mm: the Analytic would then be as good as the FVM, not better.
4. The partial-cell effect of the FVM (a cell half covered conducts like copper: -3.0 % at 0.5 mm for a pad edge half a cell off, README 9.3): a question for the cell-fill work of A12, not for this folder.
