# Analytic model on partial copper (A2): text for `docs/research.md`

**Studied and reviewed; waits for the maintainer.** Evidence: `research/analytic-partial-copper/` (README 3 to 6, review in README 9).

* **What was found.** Against a resolved 3D solution the Analytic model is -37 to +9 % on a one-layer board with a copper pad of 6 to 40 mm and -65 to -0.6 % on two layers (60 mm board, 6 mm part; -76 % with the laminate at 0.3 W/mK in the plane); -67 to +1 % on the 52 pad rows of the `gap-subdivision` baseline (mean absolute 18 %); on a board with copper everywhere it is within 1.2 %. It is too cool for pads up to 20 mm and too hot (+4.4 and +8.6 %) for one-layer pads of 30 and 40 mm.
* **Causes.** The cosine series has no pad edge (modes 300 to 600 move the peak by 8e-6): the copper enters as one weighted fill (`K0(r / lambda)`) and a uniform sheet; the split into the cost of the uniform sheet and the cost of the nodes (one per copper layer; the laminate of a one-layer board as a film) is -12.6 and -16.3 points of -28.9 % (one layer, 10 mm pad) and -52.8 and -8.5 of -61.2 % (two layers, bare second). The same board entered with one layer or two gives results up to 1.8 times apart.
* **The warning.** The program's spread warning is silent on the two worst baseline rows (thin gaps, -66.7 and -62.0 %); a second spread at the full-copper fin length leaves no board beyond 5 % silent on 119 boards and keeps the example boards silent (`INTEGRATION.md` P1).
* **A candidate** (laminate nodes and the copper as a lateral defect on the tile Green-function system) follows the FVM with the same nodes when the copper edges lie on tile edges, and is 3 to 8 % cool when they do not; it is the FVM's model solved densely (2 600 to 10 000 unknowns on a 100 x 80 mm board with a pour). The reviewer advises against adopting it.
