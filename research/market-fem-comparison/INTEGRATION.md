# Plan: comparing the program's models with market solvers (future design; the program is not touched)

**Status: deferred.** The maintainer decided to stay on scikit-fem for now; this plan is the design of the future investigation `ROADMAP.md` A47, to start when the scikit-fem work of 2.8.0 is closed. The decisions of section 3 are taken (README 7.4); the first step is README 7.5.

Nothing here changes `pcbthermal/`, tests, examples or the project documents. The work stays in `research/` and its results feed `docs/validation.md` only after the usual review and the maintainer's decision.

## 1. Proposed steps (one heavy job at a time; scratch environments outside the repository)

0. **First step** (README 7.5; 6 to 8 h): DOLFINx alone on the stripes-and-pad board of `independent-fem-extensions` study 3 and its control, own `create_box` mesh, Q2 at three levels, against the scikit-fem values; no Gmsh. Steps 2 to 5 only if it agrees within the discretisation band.
1. **Case file** (1 to 2 h): one JSON description per case, generated by the existing builders of `research/independent-fem-extensions` (hollow barrel, part over a via cluster with a plate, stripes, L outline, transient) and the read-out points of Figure 2 (part centre, barrel foot, board edge, T_min, edge heat, heat balance).
2. **Mesher** (about 6 h, shared): Gmsh **as an executable** (decided, README 7.3): our script writes a `.geo` file and calls `gmsh`; one geometry for FEniCSx and CalculiX (same nodes, different element orders). The Gmsh Python module is not imported by a committed script.
3. **FEniCSx adapter** (6 to 8 h) with tests: closed-form checks (a slab with a film, an energy balance) before any board; then the four steady cases; then the transient (5 to 6 h more).
4. **CalculiX adapter** (7 to 10 h) with the same tests; the plate/via-cluster case and the L outline first.
5. **Report** (3 to 4 h): table of the read-out points per solver and case, difference to our scikit-fem reference and to the program's five models, one figure per case; the README states the mesh levels and the unknowns. Then the reviewer; then the maintainer's decision on `docs/validation.md`.
6. **Optional** after step 5: Elmer or OpenFOAM only if the first two disagree. SimScale is not used (decision 4).

Total for steps 1 to 5: about 35 to 50 hours of agent work (estimate), peak memory of one solve below about 3 GB with CG and algebraic multigrid (estimate; a sparse LU of second-order tetrahedra would cost far more: not used), measured and refused above 3 GB as the extensions folder does; disk at most 6 GB for the scratch environments, outside the repository.

## 2. Risks

* The conda route of DOLFINx and the third-party Homebrew tap of CalculiX can break on a new macOS; keep the exact versions printed in the README.
* Thin features (the 25 um hollow barrel wall, the copper layers of 18 to 70 um) need local mesh levels; a coarse mesh would make the 'independent' result wrong for the same reason our own is hard. Mesh convergence must be shown (three levels) before any agreement is claimed.
* Anisotropic laminate and per-element conductivity differ in each tool's input; adapters must be checked on a slab case with a known answer first.
* A match proves discretisation, not physics (README 1). No sentence may suggest validation against a real board.

## 3. Decisions (taken; README 7.4)

1. **Deferred** (maintainer): no-go for now; start when the scikit-fem work of 2.8.0 is closed (`ROADMAP.md` A47).
2. **Solvers**: DOLFINx (FEniCSx, LGPL-3.0-or-later) first, CalculiX (GPL-2.0-or-later, external executable) second; Elmer only if CalculiX does not install; OpenFOAM only for a finite-volume cross-check if the two disagree.
3. **Gmsh as an executable** (GPL-2.0-or-later with a linking exception); importing the module in a git-ignored scratch process would be permitted (no distribution), but no committed script imports it.
4. **SimScale: no** for now (Free Plan non-commercial only, Community Content under a broad licence, thermal tier 'Limited' and undefined).
5. **Only free solvers in committed numbers.** No Ansys (academic terms bar benchmarking), no COMSOL (trial results may not be published; every licence bars use for developing a competing program), no other commercial tool.
6. **Budgets**: at most 3 GB resident per solve, one heavy job at a time (machine rule of CLAUDE.md), at most 6 GB of disk for the scratch environments.
