# Text for `docs/research.md`, the zonal Lumped model (open until adopted)

The zonal Lumped model (two zones per layer under a footprint with a via core) was designed and measured on the program up to 2.4.4 in `research/lumped-via-groups/` (3.6 % mean and 9.1 % maximum error of the via effect on 36 simple boards, 4.5 % and 20.2 % on 26 held-out boards, -7.1 to -0.1 % on the example board, 8.0 % and 20.8 % at a 1 mm raster). `research/lumped-zones-adoption/` prepares its adoption on the 2.4.5 wells and has written, not run, the studies of: the zones on top of the micro well and the capped cluster well (is the cluster well inside the zones a double count with the link A-C), the step response, the raster, the array across the footprint edge and the cluster off the centre, the stride decimation of the footprint potentials, and the invariants. Open: the runs, the review, the profile runner with the zones, a published case (Guenin 2004 tables, Shen 2019 equation 8, Fish 2017 chapter 7 to be obtained), a Gerber board with many vias.
