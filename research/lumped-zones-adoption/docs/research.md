# Text for `docs/research.md`, the zonal Lumped model (open until adopted)

The zonal Lumped model (two zones per layer under a footprint with a via core) was designed and measured on the program up to 2.4.4 in `research/lumped-via-groups/` and prepared for adoption on the 2.4.5 wells in `research/lumped-zones-adoption/` (via effect 4.0 % mean and 9.3 % maximum on 36 simple boards against 10.8 and 31.8 for the program; hottest temperature 2.6 and 7.2 % against 5.1 and 14.2). The checks the first review left open were run: the square-wave profile with the zones (works once the profile runner splits the source: peak 3.8 % mean and 14.3 % maximum on 93 boards against 9.6 and 28.9), a 3D solver with the barrels drawn on six cases (the zones within 1.6 % of the hottest rise, the program up to 14.0 %), the published case of Shen and others (equation (4) reproduced, the paper's CFD number not reproduced; an independent unit-cell solver and the program's models agree within 1 to 5 %), several groups under one footprint, the cost, the fallback decision across rasters and the records of the frozen-numbers test. Design and proposal: `research/lumped-zones-adoption/INTEGRATION.md`. The second review (README 10) confirmed the go with five corrections to the design (the 0.5 mm cell only for a zoned heater, so that the example board does not move; `want_barrel` needed with either macro choice; the fine share drops the vias outside the footprint box; one cell per board; a constant-power test of the profile adapter) and placed the CFD gap of the Shen case in the paper's inputs. Open: the cost on a Gerber board with many groups and on a 200 mm board at 0.5 mm, a table or one-shot profile with a zoned heater, a 3D reference with the barrel resolved, the CFD of the paper.
