# Text for `CHANGELOG.md` (Unreleased), if the zonal Lumped model is adopted

Draft with the numbers of the studies of this folder (README sections 4, 8 and 9); the final text follows the numbers of the final tree.

* **Changed**: the Lumped model represents a group of vias under a larger footprint with two nodes per layer for the footprint (the core that the vias cover and the rest), joined by a lateral link in the source layer, so that the heat of the rest of the footprint has its own way down and a way sideways to the core. The via effect of a part with a via array under a larger footprint was up to 32 % too large (the part too cool) on 36 simple boards and up to 109 % on 26 others; it is now within 9.3 % and 20.6 %; the hottest temperature of such a part is within 7.6 % of a fine-grid solution on every board measured (the program: up to 17.4 % too cool). Boards without vias, and parts whose vias form no group in the footprint or cover more than 92 % of it, give the same result as before. The temperature of a Lumped run with a part over a via array changes by about -4 K (3 % of the rise) on the test board with a 4 x 4 array under a pad.
* **Changed**: the capacities of the two footprint nodes are the sums of the capacities of their cells, so the step response and the power profiles of the Lumped model follow the same network (the square-wave peak of a part with a via core is within 5 % of the FVM's rise on the boards tried, the program's up to 21 %).
* **Added**: warnings of the Lumped model for a footprint that is elongated or whose vias are off its centre (the part may be read up to 20 % too cool in the via effect), and for a raster cell coarser than a tenth of the via core; the Lumped cell is 0.5 mm for a part over a via core.
* **Fixed**: the footprint potentials of the Lumped model were decimated by a stride above 900 cells, which made the link to the first ring 4 to 11 % short for footprints of 20 to 30 mm; they are now aggregated in blocks (this item is already in `develop`).
