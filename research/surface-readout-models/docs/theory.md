**1.2d The surface in the Analytic, the 3D solid and the transient** (proposed). The parabola of 1.2 needs the heat fluxes through the two faces of the layer. The FVM and the FDM have them cell by cell. The Analytic has a temperature field per layer and the uniform conductances of its effective sheet: the film of each outer face in series with the half copper, `h_top_eff`, `h_bottom_eff`, and the laminate conductance per gap, `g_diel`; the faces of layer 1 then read `q_top = q_src - h_top_eff theta_1`, `q_bot = -g_diel (theta_1 - theta_2)` (a one-layer board: `-h_bottom_eff theta_1`) and the parabola gives the surface: an **estimate**, flagged above `t/a` = 0.5, which does not contain the heat that a via, a block or a heatsink carries (it makes the term smaller, between `q t / 3k` and the parabola's value, never above the bound). The 3D solid has every conductance in its matrix, so with one slab through the copper the same parabola is fed with its own z links, and with several slabs the face is the first slab plus the half slab that the injection at the node does not conduct, `q_top dz / (2 k)`: **computed**, within 0.14 K of the independent surface at 8 slabs on the eight plates. For a frame of a transient run the balance of the layer is `rho c t dT_m/dt = q_top + q_bot + k t laplace(T_m)`, so the right side of `k T_zz = rho c dT/dt - k laplace(T)` is, to lowest order, `(q_top + q_bot) / t` whether the layer stores heat or spreads it: the parabola is the same formula, read from the node temperatures of the frame and the fluxes of the conductance matrix. It is exact once the heat has crossed the layer, Fourier number `alpha tau / t^2` >= 0.3 (1.03 times the exact term at 0.3), and an upper estimate before (1.30 times at 0.1, 5.66 times at 0.003): at that time the heat has not reached the bottom face and the exact term grows as the square root of the time while the parabola stays at `q t / 3k`. What is not modelled: the Lumped (no face flux), a block, the bottom face of a one-layer board's laminate, a local source in time (verified on one-dimensional boards only).

**The bound on the term** (not on the temperature): `q'' t / 2k`, with `q''` the power of the part over its footprint, is above the through-thickness term on all eight plates (1.5 to 3.1 times it) and on case 2, and above the finite-element term on 21 of 22 parts of the harness boards (not where the part sits over absent copper). The Lumped column plus this bound is **not** an upper bound of the surface: it is below the FEM surface on 8 of 22 harness parts, up to 50 K, because of the Lumped column itself.
