# Candidate entries for `docs/decisions-log.md` (taken by the reviewer, whom the maintainer follows on these items; to be prepended when the work is adopted)

* **The Lumped gets no absolute surface; a text-only limit.** Reason: the bound `q'' t / 2k` holds for the through-thickness term on all plates and 21 of 22 harness parts (measured, not proved), but the Lumped column plus the bound is below the FEM surface on 8 of 22 harness parts (up to 50 K; outlines and copper windows), so a surface number worded as an upper bound would be wrong on boards the program accepts. Rejected: offering the Lumped column plus the bound as a temperature.
* **The headline of the transient and of the Difference view stays the column**, the surface beside it. Reason: from a Fourier number of 1 the surface error equals the node error to within 0.01 K, so the surface changes nothing in a comparison between models.
* **The parabola is the rule of the Analytic (flagged 'estimate') and of a transient frame**; the 3D solid computes the face from its first copper slab plus the half slab and uses the parabola with one slab, implemented after `feature/solid3d-copper-slabs`. The frame rule of the FVM and FDM is the last item of the feature branch and may slip without blocking 2.9.0 (the `n/a` of 2.8.0 stays then).
* **The FEM's thin-copper surface term goes to the FEM work of 2.9.0**: its cause is measured (a volumetric source in a one-slab copper reads half of the exact term), the fix (a face flux for every part on an outer copper layer) moves the FEM frozen records by milli-kelvin and goes through a pending file.
