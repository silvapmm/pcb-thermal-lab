# Text for the project documents (when the proposal is adopted)

**`docs/theory.md`, section on the laminate (1.4 / 4).** On a cell with copper in every layer the laminate has no node of its own, so its edge film used to sit on the copper node at the copper temperature; the laminate near the edge is cooler than the copper (a laminate half-gap of thickness d held at the copper temperature on one face and cooled by a film h_s on its edge carries the share `eta = sum_n 8 / ((2n-1)^2 pi^2) * a_n / (h_s + a_n)`, `a_n = (2n-1) pi sqrt(k_z k_xy) / d`, 0.76 for 3.2 mm and 200 W/m2K), so the board read cold (-15 % at 200 W/m2K on 3.2 mm). The program therefore cuts the gap into sub-gaps in the cells within `ceil(d / dx) + 1` cells of the board edge when `h_side` > 0. In the FVM the film of those cell-centred laminate nodes acts behind half a cell of laminate, `g' = g / (1 + h_s dx / (2 k_xy))` (the edge film of the cut-cell treatment, which covers every node layer); the FDM's nodes are on the edge and take the film directly. Against an independent 3D solver with side films, converged on a graded mesh, the hottest rise of two-layer boards 0.8 to 3.2 mm is within 2.6 % up to 200 W/m2K; above it the four sub-gaps leave about 5 % at 500 W/m2K (use more sub-gaps and a cell of 0.25 to 0.5 mm). Source status: separation of variables (Carslaw and Jaeger) and the finite-volume boundary condition (Patankar) from memory (M); checked by an independent finite-volume solution and two 3D solvers, not by a published case. Figures: `research/laminate-edge-band/figures/fig_01`, `fig_02`, `fig_03`, `fig_07`.

**`docs/validation.md`.** Tables of README 11.2 (develop) and 11.4 (with the cut-cell film): error of the hottest rise against the converged 3D reference, as is and with the band, by thickness, `h_side` and cell.

**`docs/models.md` limits.** With an edge coefficient above 200 W/m2K the FVM and FDM read about 5 % low at 500 W/m2K on boards 3.2 mm thick with the four sub-gaps of the default laminate, and more at 2000; use `laminate_n_max` 8 or 16 with a cell of 0.25 to 0.5 mm.
