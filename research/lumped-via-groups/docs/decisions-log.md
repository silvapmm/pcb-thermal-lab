# Text for the Decisions Log of `CLAUDE.md` (to be written when the maintainer decides)

* **The via group term of the Lumped model: zones, not a series well** (`research/lumped-via-groups`, reviewed; the entry waits for the maintainer's decision): the hypothesis that the rings double-count lateral spreading inside the footprint was tested and rejected (the footprint is one node per layer; the group term outside the footprint only is the micro well alone, up to 62 % too cool on arrays that cover part of the footprint); the cause of the 18 to 32 % deficit of the series group term on dense arrays is its placement in series with the barrel, which forces the heat of the part of the footprint outside the vias to cross the sideways resistance although it has a vertical path of its own. Option C (two zones per layer, the link of the real shapes by logarithmic potentials, the micro peak and, from the review, the bump of a core hotter than the rest) is within 10 % on 36 of 36 simple boards (mean 3.6 %, max 9.1 %, at a 0.5 mm raster; 8.0 / 20.8 % at the program's 1 mm), 4.5 % mean on 26 held-out boards and -7.1 to -0.1 % on the example board with arrays under U1; options A (micro well and a warning) and B (the group term outside the footprint only, for rows) are measured for comparison. The effective-vertical-conductivity route (Shen 2019, Soleymani 2013, Fish 2017 eq. 43) is the isothermal-face limit and is 7.6 % mean and 32.0 % max when used in the zone model in place of the micro wells.
