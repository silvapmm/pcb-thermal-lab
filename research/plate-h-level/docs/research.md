# Text for `docs/research.md` (open investigation) while the decision is pending

**Plate level of a vertical face** (`research/plate-h-level/`). The program's Churchill and Chu forms against the exact boundary layer (0.5157 Ra^(1/4) at Pr 0.71, Ostrach reproduced), a 2D numerical plate and the published air data: the laminar form is the exact coefficient plus a leading edge and is within 0.6 to 2.2 % of the numerical plate for `Ra` 1e4 to 3e7; the all-range form is 5 % low at 1e4 to 1e5 and 6 to 11 % high at 1e7 to 3e7; 0.59 Ra^(1/4) is 4 to 13 % high. The rise of a vertical board moves by 2 % at most between the two forms. Open: the default (laminar or all-range), an `auto` value, the warning below `Ra` 1e3, a published finite-plate case to validate the 2D solver, 3D edge entrainment of narrow boards.
