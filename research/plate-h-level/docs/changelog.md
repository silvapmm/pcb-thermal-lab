# Text for `CHANGELOG.md` (Unreleased), to use when the work is adopted

* The natural convection of a vertical face has two named options: "Boundary layer (Churchill and Chu, laminar)" and "All Rayleigh numbers (Churchill and Chu)". The laminar form is the exact laminar boundary-layer coefficient plus the leading-edge term and agrees with a numerical solution of the finite plate to 0.6 to 2.2 % over Rayleigh numbers 1e4 to 3e7; the rise of a vertical board with computed `h` changes by at most 2 % between the two. Old projects keep the all-range form.
