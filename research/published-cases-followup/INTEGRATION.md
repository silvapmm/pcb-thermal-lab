Section 2: the barrel length `d + 0.5 t_cu` is in 2.6.0 (`viawell.barrel_length`, `BARREL_COPPER_FRACTION`; `fix/via-barrel-and-rim-uncoupling` from `research/via-contact-geometry`, A22, `docs/validation.md` 34) in the FVM, FDM, Lumped and Analytic (not the 3D solid); the via deficit of the FVM on this folder's case is now -0.30 % (was -7.46 %, the folder test). Section 3: the 3D solid slabs by thickness are in 2.6.0 (A17); the source-aware rule (A18) is open.

Nothing in `pcbthermal/`, `tests/`, `examples/` or `ico/` was edited; every proposal below was measured on the program as it is (`develop` at `fc91174a`) through its public API (the via barrel of 2 was lengthened in memory for one run, no file of the program changed). Recommendations are for the maintainer; a change to a model goes on a `fix/` or `feature/` branch with the whole battery. The reviewer's recommendation closes each section.

## 1. The Lumped model near an edge or a corner (measured, proposal)

**Measured** (README 7; the plate of Seri Lee, 100 x 100 x 1.3 mm aluminium, 25 mm source, exact thin-plate series as reference): on the mean temperature of the footprint the Lumped reads -5.3 % at the centre, -17.9 % at the edge midpoint, -21.6 % at the corner, -19 to -24 % at gaps of 2 to 6 mm from the edge or corner, recovering to -9 % at a gap of one source side (24 mm). The FVM, FDM, 3D solid and Analytic follow the exact images to 0.5 % (FDM 1.6 % on the footprint mean) at every distance.

**Measured by the review on a PCB-like board** (README 7.1; 100 x 100 mm, two layers of 35 um full copper, h 10 W/m2K, 5 x 5 mm part, against the FVM at 0.25 mm): footprint mean -2.3 % at the centre, -8.8 / -9.8 % at an edge (gap 0 / 5 mm), -11.5 / -14.0 % at a corner; the hottest point -11.6 % at the centre to -22.4 % at a corner. The capped first ring of `fix/lumped-first-ring` (`b8dd7f8`) leaves the plate unchanged and gives -6.9 / -7.2 % at the edge and -10.4 / -11.5 % at the corner: it helps, it does not close it.

**Caveat.** One plate and one PCB board, one part size each: a sample, not a bound. That the extra deficit is the missing image of the edge is a reading of the numbers, not a test of the ring construction.

**Recommendation (reviewer).** (a) A warning for the Lumped when a part lies within about one part side of a board edge or corner, quoting "about 10 % (edge) to 14 % (corner) too cool on the mean on a sample board, up to 22 % on a conductive plate; a sample, not a bound" (text only, like the existing via warnings); it can go into 2.6.0 after `fix/lumped-first-ring` is decided, and should be re-measured on that branch's tree. (b) A correction of the ring network (an image term, or rings clipped and weighted by the real outline) only after an investigation of its own (`research/ROADMAP.md` A21) with a sweep of part sizes and stack-ups; no candidate was prototyped, so no impact number exists for a fix.

## 2. Vias in the FVM and FDM: the barrel is counted over the dielectric gap only (measured, proposal)

**Measured** (README 6.3): on n x n arrays under a uniform flux with a fixed bottom, the FVM reads the array resistance 6 to 8 % low for air-filled vias (2 to 7 % for solder), grid-converged; the FDM -4 to -9 %, the 3D solid -1 to -7 %. The reference was checked: the axisymmetric unit cell and a Cartesian 3D square cell agree to 0.1 to 0.5 %.

**Cause located by the review** (README 6.4, `study_shen_vias.py` part D, 0.25 mm air vias): the deficit is per via (one via -7.46 %, an 8 x 8 array -7.40 % per via), so it is not the footprint rule at a small pitch nor the log kernel; the convergence of the heat in the planes onto the barrel is 2.4 % of the reference. The barrel conductance of the FVM is `k_cu A / d` with `d` the dielectric gap (`viawell.build`), and a copper layer is one node with no resistance through its thickness, so the barrel crosses the copper (4 x 70 um of the 1.6 mm here) for free. With the barrel counted over `d + t_cu / 2` per gap the FVM reads -0.3 / -0.5 % (pitch/3, pitch/9); over `d + t_cu` +6.6 / +6.8 %. This is the non-conservative side (an array reads better than it is). It is not the mean-potential via model: that acts in the plane.

**Estimated size elsewhere** (from `t_cu / d`, not run): a 1.6 mm two-layer board with 35 um copper about 1 %; a multilayer board with 0.2 mm prepregs and 35 um copper about 8 %; heavy copper more.

**Recommendation (reviewer).** An investigation before any change (`research/ROADMAP.md` A22): the barrel length rule (over `d + t_cu / 2`, or a resistance through the copper node that depends on how much plane is around the barrel) measured against the axisymmetric cell on a sweep of copper thickness (18 to 105 um), gap (0.1 to 1.5 mm), via diameter and filler, on two-layer and multilayer stacks, for the FVM and the FDM, and its effect on the frozen numbers (every board with vias moves). Until then, one sentence in `docs/theory.md` 2.6 and the via section of `docs/validation.md`: "the barrel is counted over the dielectric gaps only; on a 4-layer board of 70 um copper this reads a via 6 to 8 % too conductive against an independent unit cell". Open decision for the maintainer: a fix for 2.6.0 (moves numbers) or a documented limit.

## 3. Thick laminate under a small source: the layered models and the 3D solid (measured, documented limit)

README 3.3 and 5.3: for t/c >= 0.5 on bare laminate the layered models lose the through-thickness spreading (FVM and FDM -36 to -44 % of psi at t/c = 3, -4 % at 0.5), sixteen sub-gaps bring the FVM to -7 to -9 %, the 3D solid at its default four slabs is +66 to +96 % at t/c = 3 (+6 to +11 % with sixteen), the Lumped and Analytic -84 to -96 %. The totals (the temperature of the part) are within 2 to 9 % (FVM, FDM); the thin-slab cases (t/c = 0.1) within 3 % on a film of Bi 2 and within 5 % on an isothermal base. This is the known limit of `checks.check_small_part_laminate` and of the 2.6.0 items for the laminate rule and the slabs of the 3D solid (`research/ROADMAP.md` A17, A18). **Recommendation (reviewer):** none beyond checking, on the branch of those items, that the warning fires for a strip-like part (a source across the whole depth); not tested here.

## 4. The coupon base stack as a regression test (no program change)

The paper's base stack is representable with undrawn outer slots (README 4). FVM and FDM reproduce the independent solver to 0.6 % for sources of 10 to 52 % of the board, solid3d 2 %, Lumped and Analytic 4 to 5 %. **Recommendation (reviewer):** a test along the lines of `research/published-cases-followup/tests/test_studies.py::test_coupon_program_matches_the_solver_on_the_base_stack` in `tests/test_layered_vs_3d.py` (which has the Cu / FR-4 / Cu coupon) on the next `fix/` branch that touches the laminate; it exercises the undrawn-slot representation, which no test of the program does today. Low priority.

## 5. The face term of a part with a film under it (tested, small)

**Tested by the review** (README 4, `study_coupon_program.py --face-term`): `laminate.face_terms` charges `P t / (2 k A)` (all the heat of the part through the half outer layer), while the heat that the film takes directly under the part never crosses it. On the coupon with the source the size of the board (half the heat leaves through the film under it) the FVM and FDM read R_solid +2.34 %; with the term charged on the heat that goes down (share 0.495) +0.02 %; node only -2.26 %. For a 36 mm source +0.57 % becomes -0.08 % (FVM). For a real part the share that leaves directly is small (a 5 x 5 mm part at 50 K under 10 W/m2K: 1.25 % of 1 W), so the over-count is about one per cent of an already small term.

**Recommendation (reviewer):** record it as a limit of the face term in `docs/theory.md` (the face term assumes all the heat of the part enters the board); a correction (`share = 1 - h_face A T_node / P` per part) is cheap but has an effect only for parts that cover a large share of a bare-faced board; not worth a change on its own, fold it into the next change of the face terms if one comes.

## 6. Documents (text in `docs/for-the-project-documents.md`)

Corrections that do not move a number: the three inconsistencies of the sources (Yovanovich and others Table 3 mean row; Lemczyk and others case (c), Table 4 R and Table 1 duplicate row; Shen and others CFD conductivity and the Pattern II reductions); the Seri Lee corner 4.6 % above the exact plate; the via barrel limit of 2 and the face term limit of 5. These belong in `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` (done in this branch) and in the validation and theory text wherever the printed numbers are quoted (when the maintainer folds the folder into the documents).

## 7. What does not change

The program's formulas for the convection, the air, the Peaceman well, the Krogh cell and the others of the audit are not touched by these checks. The coupon, the strip cells, the 3-layer board and the plate (except the Lumped) agree with independent references within the limits stated in the README.
