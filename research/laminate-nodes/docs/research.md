# Text for `docs/research.md`: laminate nodes (S5)

**Measured and reviewed.** One construction for the laminate between copper layers (README 3): n = min(4, ceil(d / 0.4 mm)) sub-gaps in the cells with absent copper plus a margin, one link elsewhere, vertical correction, face value. Boards with copper in every cell are bit for bit the program; on two or more layers it is within 0.03 point of `bare-spot-under-source`; on one-layer boards with drawn copper it halves the shortfall of `laminate.py`; on real one-layer layouts at a 0.8 mm cell it gains only 0.2 to 2.8 points (the fill of a cell with copper finer than the cell is the error there). The SOR needs the refined columns relaxed as blocks (no point colouring exists for an even n); the real-window numbers agree with `gerber-models` R2 once its orientation is used. Open: the literature check of the construction, the earliest points of a one-layer transient, the Analytic (not helped) and the Lumped (erratic).
