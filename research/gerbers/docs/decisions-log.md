- 2026-10-23: `research/gerbers` extended (docs updated on the research branch only; `develop` and `main` untouched): the mappings were tested in the Lumped and Analytic models through the public API (Lumped inherits a better `S`, 13 % to 4.8 %; Analytic sees one uniform sheet and needs one effective conductance weighted by the heater's influence, geometric-type mean 5.9 % against 18.6 % arithmetic), and the independent reader was checked against gerbonara on its 272 test files (149 of 164 comparable agree within 3 %; four defects fixed; unsupported content is reported). No literature found on the copper fraction in lumped or cosine-series PCB models. The reader is smaller, not better: fewer features, no writing, validated on fewer files. Details: `research/gerbers/README.md` 4.16 to 4.18.
- 2026-10-22: **Gerber research branch `research/gerbers`** (maintainer: keep the squares, improve the mapping, find a way without licences, investigate the cautions, simulate; nothing in `pcbthermal/` changed). Findings (`research/gerbers/README.md`, `INTEGRATION.md`, `docs/research.md`): a Gerber converts exactly to polygons, but a 0.1 mm mask is enough; an independent reader and rasteriser needs only NumPy (`native_gerber.py`, cross-checked against gerbonara + shapely on 8 boards; unsupported features are reported); the program's fill-fraction cell conductance is 10 to 18 % too cool on real copper (7 to 43 % on regular patterns), the pixel matters little; flow-based face conductances from the mask are within 0.3 to 3.4 % in every case tested, lambda 0.5 and the geometric mean are cheaper but weaker (patterns, four layers); **the geometric mean in the program's zone formula moves a hatched zone by +26 to +45 % and four exact-value tests by 0.05 to 0.07 K, so `Zone.fill` keeps its formula and the imported copper gets its own element**, with a declared `pattern` for hatched zones; through the program's own `fvm.Network` only the conductances change (agreement 3e-12). Mesh routes (FYPA, padne) need Triangle or CGAL, whose licences are a problem for a closed product. Test boards are downloaded, never committed (GPL, CC BY-SA and unknown licences). Not decided: the stage that enters the program, the zone `pattern`, validation on a thick-copper multi-layer board and against a measurement.
- 2026-10-23: **Research scope and nets** (maintainer: only copper and drills, nets for future heat of a net): the reader is checked on copper layers and drill files only; the first drill comparison had only matched counts, a position comparison found inverted Excellon `TZ`/`LZ` and other defects (fixed); a drill file without a unit is refused rather than guessed. Nets come from the X2 attribute `TO.N` (pads and tracks; zone fills may have none, then the net of a connected piece is inferred); `research/gerbers/joule.py` solves the current of a net and gives `I²R` per cell as a heat source. The Electronics Cooling articles and the Ucamco spec could not be opened (network policy), literature status in `research/gerbers/docs/literature.md`.
- 2026-10-23: **Reader compared with the other Python tools** on files of known area (`research/gerbers/compare_readers.py`): the independent reader equals gerbonara + shapely at the pixel; the error is the pixel size, and a coverage (grey-level) mask loses far less on thin copper than a binary one (idea to test, not adopted). pygerber is strict and slower, pcb-tools failed on real boards.
- 2026-10-23: **Tracks at an angle** (maintainer asked): area, connectivity and resistance swept over 0 to 90 degrees; net membership must come from 8-connected labelling or vector geometry, not from a coarse four-connected raster; resistance of thin tracks needs 8 to 10 pixels across or the vector length and width. `joule.solve_net` got a nine-point stencil option.
- 2026-10-23: **Prior art found in the literature** (maintainer supplied the converted text of three Electronics Cooling articles): the flow-based face conductance is Sampson's method (ASME 2005, article 2017), Blackmore's tile method (2010) is the `lam 0` bound plus the arithmetic fraction. The research claims of novelty were removed; what remains ours is the validation on real boards, the pattern and multilayer studies and the program-in-the-loop check. Tests reproduce the published 40 / 0.65 W/m K example and Blackmore's tile formulas. Open: Guenin's local vertical overlap and Sampson's copper-stops-before-the-posts case as new validation scenarios; the sources not read are listed in `docs/literature.md`.
- 2026-10-23: **Third writer and the Ucamco page**: files from Ucamco's `gerber-writer` test thermal pads and arcs; the reader was right (-0.04 %) where gerbonara was 8.6 % low, and gained support for attributes written as `G04 #@!` comments. Ucamco's page confirms net names and aperture functions as standard attributes and lists a job file (thickness, finish) that could prefill the stack-up; the Ucamco example data sets and test files are the next conformance material to download (by the maintainer, not committed).
- 2026-10-23: **Conclusions of the Gerber research compiled** (`research/gerbers/CONCLUSIONS.md`): the flow-based faces are Sampson's method and the tile bound is Blackmore's; ours is the comparison on real boards, the program-in-the-loop check, the reader and the nets/Joule/angle studies; no physical measurement yet. Tests for the claims added (`tests/test_claims.py`) and the boundary-limited regime of Sampson reproduced in direction (not in size).
- 2026-10-23: **Coverage masks, all models, vias/nets** (maintainer's five questions): coverage mask recommended as the default raster; the program's zone union by maximum found to distort fine copper and to need an additive rule independently of the import; all models tested with an additive-union copy of the program (first table had used the original program by mistake and was redone); the 3D solid found unusable as a check at fine cells; vias and nets matched from the drill file, aperture functions and net attributes; an analogue of Sampson's board built (mechanism reproduced, size not). Zones as polygons do not suffice (one polygon, no holes, max union, arithmetic mix): the import needs its own mapping and data model.
