# Research summary, phase 2: the copper in the simulation models

Ready to paste into the project `docs/research.md` as a section when the maintainer adopts the work. Everything is reproducible from `research/gerber-models/` (README sections, `CONCLUSIONS.md`); nothing in `pcbthermal/` was changed.

## Open now: the copper in the simulation models

**Answers** (numbers in the README of the folder):

* **The fill fraction is biased cool**: on real one-layer copper 24 to 26 % against an independent 3D solution and 16 to 18 % against the program's own pixel run (README Review R2); 10 to 23 % in the one-sheet studies (up to 49 % across stripes). Flow-based face conductances (Sampson 2005/2017, prior art) are within 0.3 to 3.6 % of the resolved mask in every one-sheet case (README 4.4 to 4.13) and 1.1 to 1.5 % of the program's pixel run on seven real windows; lambda 0.5 within 1.0 to 3.2 % (one-sheet); coverage masks cut the error of the flow-based faces from 6.5 to 4.1 % (4.26).
* **`Zone.fill` keeps its formula; imported copper needs its own element**; zones are combined per cell with a maximum, which loses copper in abutting zones; the fix is the maximum per sample point, not an additive rule (4.27, 4.39).
* **Every model** (4.16, 4.27, 4.34, 4.35, 4.37; against the program's pixel run, `develop` `a6f822b`): FVM 21.3 to 5.6 %, FDM 26.3 to 12.7 %, Lumped 27.7 to 15.5 %, Analytic 30.4 to 22.0 % (8.8 % with one effective conductance), 3D solid 19.4 to 7.0 %; on rectangles the Lumped is 16 % and the Analytic 26 % below the FVM, the 3D solid 7 to 9 % above (two layers); the 3D solid's missing laminate under a single copper layer (346 against 174 K) is corrected in the program.
* **Import end to end** (4.31): the program's fill -15.1 % against -2.1 / -5.3 % with the imported faces (Stormduino) and -4.8 % against +7.3 / +5.2 % (own board; against an independent 3D solution -12.2 % against -1.2 %); another reader changes it by 0.01 to 0.04 %.
* **Mappings as an option** (4.38, 4.43): on eleven boards the flow-based faces, the Wiener upper bound, lambda 0.5 and the cell problem 2.3 to 2.4 % against 7.4 % for the fill fraction; `SolverOptions.copper_mapping` with `fill` as default.
* **Literature** (`docs/literature.md`): the flow-based faces are Sampson's method, the tile bound is Blackmore's, Guenin suggests two tests not yet done.

**Not validated**: any measurement of a physical board; the program's two laminate nodes are 5.5 to 13.6 % cool on patterned one-layer copper against an independent 3D solution (README Review R2); the 2D Bruggeman and Hashin-Shtrikman closed forms were written from memory and are now checked by an independent derivation and numbers (test); Sampson's board could not be rebuilt (an analogue gives a factor of 5 to 10 against his 26); the corrections of 4.39 were not run through the whole battery.

**Open decisions for the maintainer**: which stage enters the program (`INTEGRATION.md`); the zone `pattern` for hatched zones; the union of zones by sample point (a `fix/<topic>` branch; the 3D laminate fix is in the program); whether the laminate under patterned one-layer copper needs more nodes; the option `copper_mapping`.

Pictures (`../figures/`): `fig_01_copper_mapping_to_cells.png`, `fig_02_ic_board_thermal.png`, `fig_03_import_end_to_end.png`, `fig_04_import_setup.png`, `fig_05_models_our_board.png`, `fig_06_rect_board_models.png`.

* **Thin tracks and angles** (README 4.42): a 0.05 mm track already cools a 6 mm heater by 3 %; the mask recognises a track if the pixel is at most half its width; the 1 mm cells reproduce 96 to 98 % of an axis-aligned track's effect but 38 to 53 % (flow-based faces) of a 45-degree one: isolated diagonal copper is the largest residual error. **Eleven boards** (4.43): flow-based faces, Wiener upper bound, lambda 0.5 and cell problem 2.3 to 2.4 % against 7.4 % for the fill fraction (program at `a6f822b`). Guide: `REPORT.md`.
* **A detector for the diagonal faces** (README 4.48): the share of diagonal conduction decided cell by cell from the coherence of the cell tensor, 1.43 % on the 42 windows against 1.77 % (fixed share 0.5) and 1.85 % (none); right on 45-degree tracks of 0.15 to 0.3 mm, not on the thinnest and widest; thresholds chosen on the same windows. **Held-out test (README 4.50): the gate did not hold up** (1.76 % against 1.86 % with no diagonals at 1.0 mm on 60 new windows, no gain on a board never used, worse at 0.5 mm; the best share depends on the cell size): a diagnostic, not a method.
* **Diagonal couplings** (README 4.44): faces to the diagonal neighbours (flow-based axial faces plus the diagonal conduction they miss) raise the share of a 45-degree track's cooling reproduced from 37-84 % to 64-94 % (damped by 0.5) or 88-120 % (undamped) and keep the 42 real windows at 1.77 % (undamped: 3.1 %, -2.8 % bias); needs a detector of single dominant diagonal conductors before it enters the program.
