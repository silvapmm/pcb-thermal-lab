# Open points of the research branch (text moved out of docs/roadmap.md)

Notes from the work on `research/gerbers` (details in the folders named; nothing of this is in the program).

* **A measurement on a physical board.** No model has been compared with one. The protocol (one board, a heater of known electrical power, thermocouples at 1, 5, 10 and 15 mm, an infrared map, three powers, the five models, the agreement criterion fixed beforehand) is `research/models-comparison/docs/measurement-protocol.md`; nothing was built or measured.
* **A correction to the program, measured and not applied** (`research/gerber-models/README.md` 4.39): the zones of a layer combined by the maximum **per sample point** (the maximum per cell loses the copper of two zones that abut inside a cell: -8.2 % FVM, -19.1 % FDM on 0.5 mm strips). It needs a `fix/<topic>` branch off `develop` and the whole battery. The second correction found there, the laminate slab under a single copper layer in the 3D solid (346.1 K against 174.5 K), is in the program since `0ea5f07`.
* **The laminate of a one-layer board under patterned copper** (`research/gerber-models/README.md`, Review R2): against an independent 3D solution the program's two laminate nodes are 5.5 to 13.6 % cool on seven real windows (the half gap of laminate in the copper node bridges the clearances); 1.9 and 5.3 % with eight nodes on two of them. Validated before only on solid copper and pads.
* **Diagonal faces (nine-point links) did not pass the held-out test** (`research/gerber-models/README.md` 4.50): the gate of 4.48 gains 0.1 points instead of 0.4 on 60 new windows, nothing on a board never used, and is worse at 0.5 mm cells; the best diagonal share depends on the cell size. Needed before anything enters the program: a share that depends on the ratio of the cell to the track width, tested on boards with many diagonal tracks that were not used to choose it.
* **Copper mapping as an option** (`SolverOptions.copper_mapping`: `fill` by default, `lambda`, `cell_problem`, `flow`): designed in `research/gerber-models/INTEGRATION.md`, not implemented.
* **Formulas written from memory.** The 2D Bruggeman equation `k^2 - (a - b)(2f - 1)k - ab = 0` and the 2D Hashin-Shtrikman bounds are used by the mapping research. Their properties were checked numerically (`research/theory/scripts/check_mapping_formulas.py`; and by the review of `gerber-models`: each Hashin-Shtrikman bound equals the 2D Maxwell-Garnett form derived separately, Bruggeman obeys the Keller-Dykhne duality exactly, and a numerical disc in a square cell sits on the lower bound within 0.1 %, `gerber-models/tests/test_conduction.py`) and their general-dimension forms with d = 2 were seen in search summaries, but no textbook was opened: to check against Milton, *The Theory of Composites* (chapters on the Hashin-Shtrikman bounds and on duality in two dimensions) and Torquato, *Random Heterogeneous Materials*.
