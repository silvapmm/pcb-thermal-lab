# Integration

Nothing here changes the program. Proposals, each with its measurement.

1. **Validation 57, first line (the honest statement).** Proposed text: "One published transient case is reproduced: the NAFEMS 1D transient T3 (a bar with a sinusoidal end temperature), 36.60 degC at 0.08 m after 32 s. The case and the value were read in two open third-party documents that quote NAFEMS (the MOOSE and Altair OptiStruct verification pages); *The Standard NAFEMS Benchmarks* itself was not obtained. The independent reference is the exact series (36.6031 degC); the program's FEM stepper gives 36.6106 (100 elements) and 36.6050 (200 elements, a difference of 0.002 K from the series). The FVM, FDM and Analytic transients cannot hold a prescribed time-varying temperature and did not run T3; they were run on a square plate with held edges and a power step against an exact series (3). No published transient case with a heat source on a board (transient spreading resistance) could be opened." The source note in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` ([NAFEMS-T3], status N for the primary, S for the case) is already worded for this.
2. **Replace the M entries.** `[NAFEMS-T3]` in `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` changes from M to N/S (done in this branch, entries `[MOOSE-T3]`, `[Altair-T3]`, `[Lienhard-AHTT]`, `[Muzychka2023Transient]` added). `tests/test_fem_transient_closed_forms.py` could quote [Lienhard-AHTT] eqn (5.56), p. 229, for the semi-infinite closed form and the exact series (value 36.6031) in the T3 test docstring in place of "REMEMBERED"; a one-line docstring change, to be made on the `feature/` branch.
3. **Default transient stepping (a finding of the plate study, not a defect proven for the users).** The default run (logarithmic steps, implicit Euler, 20 frames) is off by about 6 % at mid-time for a plate with a 46 s time constant, in both the FVM and the FDM, independent of the mesh; Crank-Nicolson with the same frames brings 0.5 %, a linear grid with 16 substeps 0.1 to 0.2 %. Not verified: how large the GUI default number of frames is and whether the documentation already states this; proposed measurement for the maintainer's decision: the same comparison at the GUI defaults on a board with a heater, and a sentence in the user guide on the step error of the logarithmic mode. Not a change proposed without that measurement.
4. **If the maintainer wants a board-relevant published transient case**: the ITherm 2023 paper of Muzychka and Yovanovich is the candidate; the cost is the IEEE access (the maintainer's institution or purchase); the alternative is a closed form of the transient spreading of a source on a flux channel derived from the eigenseries of part B (an exact series, not a published number).
