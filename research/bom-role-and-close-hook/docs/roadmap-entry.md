# Text for `docs/versions.md` 'Planned' (item 14) when the maintainer decides

14. **BOM role and an explicit `before_close` hook** (feature, 2.6.0, after `feature/project-package`; evidence `research/bom-role-and-close-hook`): a BOM is a file role in step 1 of the Gerber import (found by its columns, not by its name); read once, it proposes the Role of each part in step 2 (`source` for resistors, inductors and ferrites, `component` for ICs, transistors, diodes and LEDs; the user's edit always wins) and marks the parts it says are not populated (DNP/DNI). A BOM never sets a power and never moves a part. The import window gets a `before_close` list (run once, before the session releases its ZIP, exceptions logged, no veto) and the session a `before_release` list, so that the package capture no longer replaces `session.close`. About 35 hours. Not included: a power list by designator (separate item), a veto from the import window, a datasheet lookup by MPN.
