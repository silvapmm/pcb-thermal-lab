# Decisions log entry (for `CLAUDE.md`, when adopted)

* **SOR with the mean-potential via couplings** (research `sor-with-via-couplings`): the failure of the program's red-black SOR with the treatment of `point-via-convergence` was not a property of SOR but of `solve_sor` iterating the stencil with the diagonal of the assembled matrix (the via off-diagonals dropped, its diagonal kept: a leak to the ambient); the matrix is symmetric positive definite and not an M-matrix, not diagonally dominant. Block SOR (the unknowns of every via solved exactly after the two colours) reaches the direct solution to 1e-8 K in the sweeps of the board without vias and is stable for any via stiffness; the explicit via term in the residual diverges for stiff vias or fine cells; Woodbury, SSOR-CG and the point link were measured and rejected. Choice left to the maintainer: block SOR or refusing SOR on boards with vias.
