# Decisions log entry (for `CLAUDE.md`, when adopted)

* **SOR with the mean-potential via couplings** (research `sor-with-via-couplings`): the failure of the program's red-black SOR with the treatment of `point-via-convergence` was not a property of SOR but of `solve_sor` iterating the stencil with the diagonal of the assembled matrix (the via off-diagonals dropped, its diagonal kept: a leak to the ambient); the matrix is symmetric positive definite and not an M-matrix, not diagonally dominant. Block SOR (the unknowns of every via solved exactly after the two colours, relaxed by the same omega, the blocks coloured so that coupled blocks move in turn) reaches the direct solution to 1e-8 K in at most the sweeps of the board without vias, is stable for any via stiffness and converges for 0 < omega < 2 (energy argument; the unrelaxed block step of the first version was up to 2.8 times slower on dense via arrays); the explicit via term in the residual diverges for stiff vias or fine cells; Woodbury, SSOR-CG and the point link were measured and rejected. Choice left to the maintainer: block SOR or refusing SOR on boards with vias.
