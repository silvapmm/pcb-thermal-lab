# Decisions of `INTEGRATION.md` 7, answered by the reviewer

**Research, not legal advice.** These answers rest on the evidence of the folder (`README.md` 2 to 6: the legal texts and licence pages read, the pilot of three notes, the checker and its limits) and on the papers rules of `CLAUDE.md` 7 and the licence rules of `CLAUDE.md` 5 (third-party material is never committed). They are engineering and project-hygiene choices; they do not say what a court would decide. By the maintainer's standing rule his open decisions on this folder are put to the reviewer and he goes with the answer; decision 6 needs money and the maintainer's own judgement, so it stays with him.

| # | Question | Decision | Reason |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Adopt the principle (own-words notes in git, everything that reproduces expression local in `papers/`)? | **Yes.** Write it as a rule in `CLAUDE.md` 5 and 7 on a `docs/` branch (one sentence each, a pointer to `INTEGRATION.md` 2 for the table by class). | It is already the practice of `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` and `docs/theory.md` (README 1); ideas, methods and facts are on the free side of the line (17 USC 102(b), README 2.1, R) and expression is not, so stating the line costs nothing and prevents the one costly accident: a copy in a public history that is never rewritten (README 5). |
| 2 | Per-key note files or keep the bibliography entries as they are? | **Pilot of 20 files** under `research/theory/notes/`, as `INTEGRATION.md` 6.2 lists, then a review of the pilot decides whether to continue. `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` stays the index. No file is committed before the checker of `INTEGRATION.md` 4 covers it, with the four limits of README 6.4 either fixed or written into the note review. | Files add structure and a place for a test but also more text, the risk the policy guards against; three notes are too few to judge the cost (one to two hours each with the page check of README 6.2) or to calibrate the thresholds on short pastes (README 4, not calibrated). |
| 3 | Who signs "own words"? | **The reviewer**, after reading every note against the source text (the image-checked transcription; a crop of the page image for items cited from `machine` pages), with an image re-check of the cited equations and numbers on a sample (1 in 3, plus every flagged note), recorded in `research/REVIEW.md`; an unsigned note is a draft and is not committed. *Amended by the reviewer, method B, adopted by the maintainer.* | The checker sees copied words only, never close paraphrase (README 6.4 item 4); the review of the pilot found errors of fact in all three notes that no tool would have found (README 6.2). |
| 4 | Quotation limit? | **At most one quotation per note, at most 25 words**, with the page; the checker must test inline quotations too (README 6.4 item 2). Standards and vendor documents: **no quotation**, only values with their clause or document id. | No text read gives a number ("extent not beyond the purpose", Berne Art. 10(1), Directive 2001/29 Art. 5(3)(d)); 25 words is a conservative house rule, and the pilot needed none. Standards bodies say even small reproductions need permission (README 2.2). |
| 5 | Licence column? | **Read the landing page of each pilot key before its note is written**, record the SPDX value or `all rights reserved` with the URL read. Until read, `not checked` counts as `all rights reserved`: no relaxation for an open licence is claimed on a classifier guess or on the licence of another version (arXiv preprint versus journal, README 2.2). | 281 of 290 entries are `not checked` (README 2.2); the classifier is about 20 % wrong outside the strict classes (README 3); the Jaffer case shows the preprint and the journal version carry different licences. |
| 6 | A lawyer before a commercial version or a bundle that ships notes? | **For the maintainer** (it costs money and only he decides a commercial version or a bundle). Reviewer's opinion: not needed for the research-only notes in an MIT repository under decisions 1 to 5; needed before any commercial version, any bundle that ships the notes, or a rightholder notice. | Portuguese art. 75 wording, EU quotation case law and the per-paper licences were not verified (README 5, 7); none of this has been read by a lawyer. |
| 7 | Machine drafts? | **Forbidden**: a note whose prose is drafted by a model asked to summarise or paraphrase the paper, its page images or its transcription. **Allowed**: reading the local transcription to understand; a model writing or formatting the prose from a fact sheet of our verified findings (formulas, numbers, conditions, page ids; no sentence of the source) with the source closed. The OCR output stays in `papers/`. *Amended by the reviewer, method B, adopted by the maintainer.* | Owning a model's output gives no right over the work it was fed (Anthropic terms L.1, Gemini terms, README 2.4, R); a model given the whole paper tends to follow its order and wording, the close-paraphrase failure the checker cannot see (README 5, 6.4). |

**Consequence.** Decisions 1 to 5 and 7 are adopted under the maintainer's standing rule; they enter the project only through a `docs/` branch (the `CLAUDE.md` rules) and the F10 pilot (the notes and the checker extensions of `INTEGRATION.md` 4 on a branch of their own). Decision 6 waits for the maintainer and blocks nothing until a commercial version or a bundle is planned.
