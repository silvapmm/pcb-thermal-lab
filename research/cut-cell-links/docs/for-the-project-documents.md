# Text for the project documents when the work is adopted (not adopted yet)

**`docs/versions.md`, chapter 'Planned', item 'Cut cells treated by their real shape' (replace the option P1 text):** after the exact board cover, the lateral links of the FVM are `a S dy/dx` with `a` the open fraction of the shared face (the aperture), nodes at the cell centres, and the edge film is in series with the conduction from the centre to the closest outline point, `g = g0 / max(1 + g0 d / (S L), 0.25)`. Evidence `research/cut-cell-links` (README 2, 4): the spread of the peak over the position of the outline falls from 0.06 to 3.7 K to 0.03 to 0.14 K at 1 mm (`h_side` 10 to 2000 W/m2K), the sliver anomaly of the present links (+3.5 K at `h_side` 200 for an edge cell of cover 0.02) disappears, K stays symmetric positive definite with no positive off-diagonal entry. Decisions and open points: `research/cut-cell-links/INTEGRATION.md` 8.

**`docs/theory.md` 2 (FVM), on adoption:** the link of two cells across a cut is the sheet conductance over the aperture times the face length divided by the distance between the nodes (one cell width); the film on the outline is the series of the conduction `d/(S L)` and the film `1/g0` (the Robin condition of a cut cell written as in Barrett, Fogelson and Griffith 2022, eq. 12 to 14, after Bochkov and Gibou 2019, not read). Pictures: figures 1 and 2 of the folder. Sources: [SchwartzEtAl2006] R pp. 1 to 16, [JohansenColella1998] R pp. 1 to 18, [CalhounLeVeque2000] R pp. 143 to 151, [BarrettEtAl2022] R, [BochkovGibou2019] N.
