# Text for the Decisions Log of `CLAUDE.md`, to use when the work is adopted

* **Channel effect between close fins** (investigation `research/fin-channel`, branch `research/fin-channel`): the correlations of Elenbaas, Churchill, Bar-Cohen and Rohsenow and Raithby and Hollands were read (the Waterloo review, equations 8 and 20 to 22; the originals of 1942 and 1984 were not obtained) and reproduced against an independent numerical channel; measured optimum spacings and the shape of `Q(s)` of published arrays reproduced from 6 mm; on the example block (FVM and 3D solid, 40 mm channels, 3 W) the channel effect raises the rise by +1.9, +4.7, +11.8, +27, +55 % at 6.5, 5.5, 4.5, 3.5, 2.5 mm gaps (the full composite coefficient gives less, -0.1 to +48 %, because its plate level is 7 % above the Churchill and Chu plate; corrected by the review); the warning of 4 mm misses more than half of the cases that lose 10 % of the finned face and is replaced in the proposal by a rule on `Ra_b < 90`; a correction `h_fin = phi(Ra_b) h_face` is proposed as an option for vertical boards only. Not validated: open-front arrays (3 mm), horizontal boards, radiation between the fins. Decided by the maintainer: (to be filled).
