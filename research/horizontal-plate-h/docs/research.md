# Text for docs/research.md (open investigation: the horizontal faces)

**C8, the plate level of a horizontal face** (`research/horizontal-plate-h/`, reviewed). Read: Schulenberg's report (KIT; his formulas are local values at the centre of the plate), Jaffer (2023), Lienhard's pages on horizontal plates; not obtained: the originals of Fujii and Imura, Al-Arabi and El-Riedy, Raithby and Hollands, Lloyd and Moran (abstract only). The program's upper-face correlation lies between the published levels over the boards' `Ra` (Jaffer 3 to 8 % above it, Raithby and Hollands up to 20 % below); its lower-face correlation has the wrong exponent and no shape dependence (29 % low to 43 % high depending on the shape and `Ra`; for squares above `Ra` 1e4 it gives more cooling than every published form). The rise moves by -2.7 to +1.3 % with the published forms (-3.8 to +3.3 % without radiation). An independent solver reproduces Schulenberg's local theory at the centre of the lower face within 4 % but not the measured upper face. Open: the coupling of the two faces of a free board (a fall of the upper-face coefficient in a steady symmetric two-dimensional solution, size not established), the originals of the measurements, a three-dimensional unsteady solution or a measurement of a thin horizontal plate heated on both faces, and the radiation between fins hanging down (C9).
