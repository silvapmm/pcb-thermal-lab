# Text for docs/research.md (open investigation: the horizontal faces)

**C8, the plate level of a horizontal face** (`research/horizontal-plate-h/`). Read: Schulenberg's report (KIT), Jaffer (2023); not obtained: Fujii and Imura, Al-Arabi and El-Riedy, Raithby and Hollands, Lloyd and Moran (abstract only). The program's upper-face correlation lies 3 to 7 % below the published fit over the boards' `Ra`; its lower-face correlation has the wrong exponent and no shape dependence (29 % low to 43 % high depending on the shape and `Ra`). The rise moves by under 3 % with other published forms. Open: the coupling of the two faces of a free board (a fall of the upper-face coefficient in a steady symmetric two-dimensional solution, size not established), the originals of the measurements, a three-dimensional unsteady solution or a measurement of a thin horizontal plate heated on both faces, and the radiation between fins hanging down (C9).
