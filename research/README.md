# research/

Studies that are not part of the program, one folder per investigation (the index is [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)). The rules are in `CLAUDE.md`, section "Research branches"; in short:

* **Layout of a folder**: the root holds what is read (`README.md` with the numbered results, `INTEGRATION.md` or `IMPLEMENTED.md`, `LOG.md`); `figures/`, `docs/`, `tests/`; `scripts/` holds all the executable Python (run from the repository root, `study_<question>.py` and `make_figures.py`); `lib/` only when the studies share code.
* **On a research branch** the project documents (`README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/`) are not edited; the text that would go into them when the work is adopted is kept in `research/<topic>/docs/`.
* **Data**: downloaded boards, caches and outputs go in `data/` and `out/`, git-ignored and never committed (a fetch script is kept instead); the licences of what is downloaded are respected.

**Pictures.** Figures of an investigation are kept in `research/<topic>/figures/`, named `fig_NN_topic.png` with NN in order of creation, generated by a script in the folder (`scripts/make_figures.py`), and linked from the README files with a relative Markdown link, `![caption](figures/fig_NN_topic.png)`, followed by an italic line with the figure number and file name. They are committed on purpose; other generated outputs stay out of git.

**Licence of the pictures.** In `gerbers/`, figures 01, 02 and 04 to 06 come from our own synthetic boards (`lib/our_board.py` for 04 to 06), and figure 03 from the Stormduino board (MIT, https://github.com/Chromico/stormduino, with attribution in its caption). The earlier versions of figures 02 and 03 rendered boards of gerbonara's test resources (`charliewatch-v2`, `chibi`) whose licences could not be confirmed at their sources, so they were replaced (they remain in the git history). Boards used only to compute statistics stay in the git-ignored `data/` and are never committed. Before adding a figure of a third-party board, read its licence at the source and note it here and in the caption.
