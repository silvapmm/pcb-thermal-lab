# research/

Studies that are not part of the program, one folder per investigation (the index is [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md); the plans, the open questions to investigate, are [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md); the review register is [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md)). The rules are in `CLAUDE.md`, section "Research branches"; in short:

* **Layout of a folder**: the root holds what is read (`README.md` with the numbered results, ending with what was read and what was not verified; `INTEGRATION.md` with the design for entering the program, when the work is not in it yet); `figures/`, `docs/`, `tests/`; `scripts/` holds all the executable Python (run from the repository root, `study_<question>.py` and `make_figures.py`); `lib/` only when the studies share code.
* **On a research branch** the project documents (`README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/`) are not edited; the text that would go into them when the work is adopted is kept in `research/<topic>/docs/`.
* **Data**: downloaded boards, caches and outputs go in `data/` and `out/`, git-ignored and never committed (a fetch script is kept instead); the licences of what is downloaded are respected.

**Pictures.** Figures of an investigation are kept in `research/<topic>/figures/`, named `fig_NN_topic.png` with NN in order of creation, generated by a script in the folder (`scripts/make_figures.py`), and linked from the README files with a relative Markdown link, `![caption](figures/fig_NN_topic.png)`, followed by an italic line with the figure number and file name. They are committed on purpose; other generated outputs stay out of git.

**Diagrams and illustrations.** Besides the figures of the studies (`make_figures.py`, which need data), every folder has pictures that explain the situation: drawings of the thing itself (cross-sections, copper patterns, the cases compared) and flow charts or networks, in `scripts/make_diagrams.py` (no data, no program; the shapes of `research/diagram_kit.py`). Both kinds of picture go to `figures/` with **one numbering** and are linked from the README of the folder.

**Licence of the pictures.** In `gerber-import/`, `gerber-models/` and `gerber-nets/` (the Gerber research, split in three), every figure comes from our own synthetic boards (`lib/our_board.py` and `lib/rect_board.py` of `gerber-import`) or from a window of the Stormduino board (MIT, https://github.com/Chromico/stormduino, with attribution in the caption of `gerber-models/figures/fig_01_copper_mapping_to_cells.png`). The earlier versions of two figures rendered boards of gerbonara's test resources (`charliewatch-v2`, `chibi`) whose licences could not be confirmed at their sources, so they were replaced (they remain in the git history). Boards used only to compute statistics stay in the git-ignored `gerber-import/data/` (shared by the three folders) and are never committed. Before adding a figure of a third-party board, read its licence at the source and note it here and in the caption.
