# Text for `docs/validation.md` 21.1, "The error of the 2-layer boards"

(Replaces the paragraph that quotes numbers from a script that was run from a scratch directory. Every number here is printed by a script of `research/gap-subdivision/` and stored in its `baseline/`.)

A 2-layer board has two copper nodes with one gap between them, and the gap is one vertical resistance. That is exact where the copper is complete on both sides of the gap: on uniform boards (copper in every cell of every layer) the FVM is within -0.72 to +1.04 % and the FDM within -1.31 to +0.19 % of an exact series solution of the layered plate (2 to 8 layers, gaps 0.1 to 3.2 mm, copper 17.5 to 105 um, sources 2 to 28 mm, cells 0.25 to 2 mm, h 2 to 500 W/m2K), the Analytic within -0.91 to +0.02 %, the 3D solid within 0 to +1.33 %, the Lumped within -1.70 to +4.14 %; cutting the gap into more nodes changes those results by 0.0 to 0.26 points with a 6 mm source.

The shortfall of 2 to 8 % recorded for 2-layer boards belongs to **boards with bare laminate around a copper pad and a thick gap**, not to every 2-layer board. Against a converged independent 3D solution (a 3 mm source on the top layer, a square copper pad of the given side, copper below, 30 mm board, 1.6 mm gap, 0.5 mm cell, h 10 W/m2K) the FVM is -10.5 % (6 mm pad), -4.7 % (10 mm), -2.3 % (14 mm), -0.9 % (20 mm), -0.3 % (26 mm); with the pad at 10 mm and the gap at 0.1, 0.4, 0.8, 1.6, 3.2 mm it is +0.3, -0.2, -1.6, -4.7, -10.2 %. The number of layers does not matter (the same gaps on 3 and 4 layers: -4.7 %), nor does the bottom (full, empty, the same pad: within 1.3 points). The FDM, the Lumped and the Analytic are lower on the same boards (FDM -7 to -16 %, Lumped -8 to -14 %, Analytic -13 to -46 % with a 6 to 10 mm pad), the 3D solid is 0.4 to 5.1 % above (the in-plane error of the 0.5 mm cell, which the layered models share, plus its four slabs per gap).

The cause is the heat that runs sideways in the bare laminate across the thickness of the gap: the laminate under the edge of the pad is hotter at the top of the gap than at the bottom, and one node per layer holds half a gap at one temperature. Probes on the 10 mm pad board: with the in-plane conductivity of the laminate turned down to 0.01 W/mK the error is +0.2 %; with the through conductivity at 0.1 and 1 W/mK it is -11.8 and -1.2 %; extra nodes only under the pad change nothing (-4.7 to -4.4 %); the reading of the surface at the copper node moves the result by 0.04 points. Cutting the gap into n sub-gaps in the cells that show laminate removes the through-thickness part of the error like 1 / n (-5.9 % of the gap alone at n = 1, -3.3, -1.9, -1.0 at n = 2, 4, 8 with equal sub-gaps; -1.5 and -0.5 % at n = 4 and 8 with a 2:1 grading); the total error is also moved by the in-plane error of the cell, which has the other sign (+1.3 % at 0.5 mm on this board), so the total is -0.6 % at n = 4 and +0.25 % at n = 8 at that cell and is not the measure of the gap. Not implemented: a decision of the maintainer, `research/gap-subdivision/INTEGRATION.md` P2.

Screen: a heater on a copper footprint smaller than about 8 gap thicknesses (about 12 when the far side of the gap has no copper) reads 3 % or more cool in the FVM (about 10 % at 3 to 4 gap thicknesses; more at a higher h or a finer cell) and by more in the other models.
