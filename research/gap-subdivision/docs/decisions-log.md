- **The gap of multilayer boards (A1, `research/gap-subdivision/`)**: measured on copies, against an exact layered series (no mesh; reproduces two published solutions) and a converged graded 3D solver, that boards with copper in every cell need no change (FVM and FDM within about 1 %, the gap nodes move the result by 0.0 to 0.26 points) and that the 2-layer shortfall belongs to bare laminate around a copper pad on a thick gap (-10.5 to -0.3 % for pads of 6 to 26 mm, 1.6 mm gap); the cause is the lateral heat flow in the bare laminate across the gap. Extra nodes in the cells that show laminate remove the gap error like 1 / n; subdividing every gap of every board is not recommended. The baseline of the program on simple boards is `research/gap-subdivision/baseline/`, re-run with `scripts/rerun_baseline.py`, the common reference for the changes of 2.5.0.
