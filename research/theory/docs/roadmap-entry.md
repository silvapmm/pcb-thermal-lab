### Open points of the research branch (so that they are not forgotten)

Notes from the work on `research/gerbers` (details in the folders named; nothing of this is in the program).

* **A measurement on a physical board.** No model has been compared with one. The protocol (one board, a heater of known electrical power, thermocouples at 1, 5, 10 and 15 mm, an infrared map, three powers, the five models, the agreement criterion fixed beforehand) is `research/models-comparison/docs/measurement-protocol.md`; nothing was built or measured.
* **Two corrections to the program found by the Gerber work: one applied, one still open** (`research/gerber-models/README.md` 4.39): the zones of a layer combined by the maximum **per sample point** (today the maximum per cell loses the copper of two zones that abut inside a cell; 5 to 15 % cool on real copper) and the laminate slab under a single copper layer in the 3D solid (346.1 K against 174.0 K). The second is **corrected in the program** (the 3D solid builds the laminate of a one-layer board as four FR-4 slabs, `docs/validation.md` 20); the first still needs a `fix/<topic>` branch off `develop` and the whole battery.
* **Diagonal faces (nine-point links) did not pass the held-out test** (`research/gerber-models/README.md` 4.50): the gate of 4.48 gains 0.1 points instead of 0.4 on 60 new windows, nothing on a board never used, and is worse at 0.5 mm cells; the best diagonal share depends on the cell size. Needed before anything enters the program: a share that depends on the ratio of the cell to the track width, tested on boards with many diagonal tracks that were not used to choose it.
* **Copper mapping as an option** (`SolverOptions.copper_mapping`: `fill` by default, `lambda`, `cell_problem`, `flow`): designed in `research/gerber-models/INTEGRATION.md`, not implemented.
* **Formulas written from memory.** The 2D Bruggeman equation `k^2 - (a - b)(2f - 1)k - ab = 0` and the 2D Hashin-Shtrikman bounds are used by the mapping research. Their properties were checked numerically (`research/theory/scripts/check_mapping_formulas.py`); the Hashin-Shtrikman bound was since confirmed in Milton, *The Theory of Composites*, equation (23.5) with d = 2, and the 2D duality in its chapter 3 (read, `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`); the Bruggeman form is still to check (Torquato, *Random Heterogeneous Materials*).
* **Sources to open** (status in `research/theory/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`): the textbooks Patankar (1980), Incropera and DeWitt, Carslaw and Jaeger, Milton (2002), Torquato (2002), Briggs and others (2000); the papers Hashin and Shtrikman (J. Appl. Phys. 33, 1962: the conductivity paper; the 1963 J. Mech. Phys. Solids paper is the elastic one), Keller (1964), Dykhne (1970), Churchill and Chu (1975), Churchill (1977, to confirm that the program's blending rule is its rule), Culham and Yovanovich (ITherm 1998; since found in the open archive of its authors and read as images, pages 460 to 466), Lush (Electronics Cooling, May 2004; since found and opened through a page converter, no quantitative validation), Lee and others (1995, the citation itself could not be confirmed), Elenbaas (1942), Bar-Cohen and Rohsenow (1984), the Hollands correlation; the standards JEDEC JESD15-3 and the JESD51 family, IPC-4761, IPC-2152, ASTM D5470, ASME V&V 20, and the Ucamco Gerber specification (latest revision 2026.05 of 19 May 2026 on the Downloads page, 2024.05 before it; take the PDF from the Downloads page and read the revision on its cover, the announcement page links a file named 2022-02), JESD51-3 and JESD51-7 by number on jedec.org (free after registration), and the byline of the multigrid primer (since confirmed: McCormick and Tamstorf, arXiv:2605.17828). The reference compilation received from the maintainer is kept in `research/theory/docs/reference-compilation.md`; it settles several citations (Churchill 1977 is AIChE J. 23 (1), 10-16; Culham and Yovanovich ITherm'98 pp. 460-467) and leaves open: the exponent and sign rule of the Churchill blend, the optimum spacing of Bar-Cohen and Rohsenow and which Hollands paper the notes cite (the three averaging schemes of Culham and Yovanovich and the Milton chapter on 2D duality were read since).
