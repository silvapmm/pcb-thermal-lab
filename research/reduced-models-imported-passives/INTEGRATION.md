# Integration: what to do with the reduced models on imported copper

Recommendation first, evidence in `README.md`. Nothing here is implemented; the program is untouched. Reviewed (README 6): items 1 to 4 corrected and items 5 and 6 added by the review.

*(audit 2.6.1, checked against the program of 2.6.0; 'Nothing here is implemented' is stale.)* **Done, 2.5.1** (`fix/reduced-model-warning-text`): item 3, the warning states the cause and the sample of 42 parts (`copper.REDUCED_MODEL_WARNING`), and item 5 (b), the heater-cell warning on imported copper names the floor of 0.25 mm and the 0402 example of 84.6 K at 1 mm against 201 K at 0.25 mm (`checks.IMPORTED_CELL_FLOOR_MM`, `copper.SMALL_PART_CELL_NOTE_TEXT`); item 5 (c) is met by the same text (it recommends the floor, not the unreachable side / 4). **Done in part, 2.6.0** (`fix/lumped-first-ring`): item 6, the cause of the Lumped's own deficit on a uniform sheet was found in `research/lumped-ring-uniform-deficit` and the first ring is capped at the equivalent radius; `docs/validation.md` 31.2 says the RP2040 imported mean moved from -59.6 to -56.8 % and that the warning numbers were not re-measured (`docs/versions.md` 'After 2.6.0' item 10). Items 1 and 2 hold (warn, never refuse; no fill-based threshold). **Not planned**: item 4 (a correction that reads the stack around the part) and 5 (a) (a finer floor or a windowed refinement), both open as `research/ROADMAP.md` E13 (b) and (c).

## 1. Recommendation

1. **Keep the Lumped and Analytic models available on imported boards, and change the warning so that it says the cause and quotes the measured ranges** (text only, no number moves, frozen numbers unchanged). Do not refuse: the best cases (a large part in plain copper) are 17 to 35 % cool, a usable screening number, and a refusal would take the quick board-level view away for no measured gain in safety, since the warning already tells the user that the finite-volume model is the result.
2. **Do not add a warning threshold on the local copper fill, the distance to a copper edge or a "part on bare laminate" test as a predictor of the error.** Measured on 42 parts of three boards (README 3.3), none of them separates the good from the bad cases: the fill variables and the distance to bare laminate have an AUC of 0.65 to 0.78 for "more than 50 % too cold" and a Spearman correlation of 0.07 to 0.31 with the error. The best variable, the size of the part (AUC 0.87), is not a rule either: the size classes overlap (Lumped -81 to -52 % below 1.5 mm, -82 to -25 % from 1.5 to 3 mm, -56 to -25 % above 3 mm), and big parts of CO60 are 50 to 56 % cool. Every one of the 42 parts measured is at least 17 % cool (a sample, not a bound). The distance to bare laminate, as measured, is the gap between the part's own pads for 30 of the 42 parts, so a copper-edge test was not evaluated, only not supported (review, README 6.3).
3. **Replace the sentence "these models do not read the imported copper" of `copper.REDUCED_MODEL_WARNING`** (`docs/validation.md` 27.7 and the user guide quote it) by the cause and the ranges, see `docs/warning-text.md` for the proposed wording. The present text already quotes the whole-board figures (27 to 42 % on U1 and U3, up to 75 % for passives) next to the 1 to 16 % of the 24 mm windows; what is wrong in it is the sentence "these models do not read the imported copper" (they read its cell fill) and the missing cause. The review rewrote the proposal (size by package, not by the square root of the paste hull; no unmeasured track width; "a sample, not a bound" kept). **Recommended for 2.5.x** (text only, frozen numbers unchanged).
4. **Model change: not recommended as it stands.** A pad-scale correction of the Lumped and Analytic rise, computed from the top-layer mask around the part (a pixel resistor network in a 16 mm window with a leaking fin, 0.1 s per part), cut the mean absolute error from 41 to 60 % to 12 to 22 % on RP2040 and oswlight (fin length 0.5 to 0.75 mm, chosen by a sweep on RP2040 and carried to oswlight), and left it at 57 to 62 % on CO60, where the pads are tied through the second layer (README 3.4). It has one empirical parameter, ignores the second layer and the vias, and would move the Lumped and Analytic numbers of imported boards. The open question for `research/ROADMAP.md`: a correction that reads the whole stack (both layers and the vias) around the part, or the Lumped model fed with the pixel network in a window around each part; and the cause of the Lumped's own 2 to 30 % deficit on a uniform sheet (README 3.1 point 3).

5. **The Auto cell: no change needed for the case that was feared.** Auto already refines for a powered small part: a quarter of its narrow side, at least 0.25 mm; the 1 mm cap for imported copper is a ceiling (README 6.2: RP2040 and oswlight 0402 parts get 0.25 mm, estimated FVM peak 1.3 to 1.6 GB; CO60 gets 0.40 mm, 7.9 GB, accepted by the guard on a 16 GB machine). A user following Auto reads C3 at 201 K, not 85 K. What remains, for the maintainer: (a) Auto's floor of 0.25 mm leaves an 0402 2.6 cells across and the reference still moves 15 to 30 % from 0.5 to 0.25 mm; a floor of 0.125 mm when memory allows would cost about 7 GB on a 52 x 51 mm board (4.5 GB on 36 x 36 mm) and was not run, so its gain is unknown: **not recommended without a study** (a windowed refinement around the powered part is the cheaper candidate, ROADMAP E13); (b) when the user types a coarse cell, the existing check says only "the smallest side is only N cells wide"; adding, for imported copper, that the FVM read C3 2.3 times too cool at 1 mm (84.6 against 195.6 K at 0.5 mm) is a **text-only option for 2.5.x**, recommended; (c) the same check fires at Auto's own 0.25 mm and recommends 0.16 mm, which Auto cannot give: harmless but confusing; recommended to word it "Auto's smallest cell" when the cell equals the floor.
6. **The Lumped's own deficit on a uniform sheet** (2 to 16 % on RP2040, 7 to 13 % on oswlight, 9 to 30 % on CO60, worst on the smallest parts; the Analytic on the same sheet and rectangle is -8 to +5 %): a study of the ring construction for a source smaller than the cell, **for 2.6.0** (ROADMAP E13); it does not change the 2.5.x recommendation, because on imported copper the pad structure dominates.

## 2. What does not change

* Frozen numbers (`tests/regression`): no change. Drawn boards do not go through any of the new code; imported boards are not in the frozen set.
* The FVM, FDM and 3D solid, the memory guard and the import window: no change.
* Nothing in `pcbthermal/`, `tests/`, `examples/`, `docs/` is touched by this branch.

## 3. Cost

* Warning text (item 3): one constant in `pcbthermal/copper.py`, the strings in `docs/validation.md` 27.7 and the user guide where the warning is quoted, `tests/test_imported_copper.py` for the new words. About an hour, no risk to numbers.
* Item 4 (if ever): a new function in `copper.py` or `lumped.py`, the window solve (sparse, 40 000 unknowns per part, a tenth of a second), tests against the closed forms of `lib/constriction.py` (strip, disc, fin), a re-freeze of nothing (imported boards are outside the frozen set) but a new validation table; several days with the reviewer.

## 4. Risks and what is not known

* The FVM reference itself is not converged for 1 mm parts at 0.25 mm (README 3.2, confirmed with a fixed pixel in README 6.2: the cell, not the masks): the errors in this folder are **lower bounds** of the error against a converged reference, and the sign of the missing part is "even cooler".
* Nothing was compared with a measurement of a physical board.
* The three boards are Gerber exports of hobby and small-product designs (two layers, one four-layer); a board with solid planes under every small part was not tried and could be nearer the "large part in plain copper" rows (17 to 35 % cool); that is a guess, not a finding.
